# BL-16 LV-1, debug pass — 2026-11-24 (pitch-sign revert)

### Debug Report

### Observed Issue

Third re-fly, after `5105b9a8` (`LAW_DIR.pitch = -1.0`, reasoning "arg 1100 positive is stick
forward/nose down"): after take the law asked nose-up (`t_p` rising, pitch below `theta_d`) and
drove `out_pitch`/arg 1100 from 0.19 toward -1 (full forward); nose fell -0.5° → -86° in 8 s, load
factor 1.0 g → 0.5 g (push-over), speed rising, aircraft tumbled. User confirmed the nose went
down. Recorded in `docs/acceptance/2026-11-24-sortie-feedback.md`, "Third re-fly".

### Hypothesis

`5105b9a8`'s premise — arg 1100/command 2001 positive = stick forward/nose down — is wrong.
Positive is stick **aft**/nose up, matching the law's own convention (`SIGN.pitch = 1.0` already
agrees with the law domain), so `LAW_DIR.pitch` should be `1.0`, not `-1.0`. With `LAW_DIR.pitch =
-1.0`, the pitch loop is positive feedback: the law's correction gets inverted on the way to the
written command, which is exactly the observed runaway.

### Evidence

- Correlation of `a1100` with pitch **acceleration** (`d(thetadot)/dt`, lag ~2 samples) is
  **positive** (+0.25..+0.64) in every hand-flown and most active session of
  `data_flight_trace.log` and `refly3_flight_trace.log` (gitignored; the sortie-feedback doc has
  the readings).
- Steady trim at each take: higher speed → lower `a1100` (40 m/s → 0.19, 58 m/s → 0.04) — faster
  cruise needs more forward stick, so lower `a1100` is forward, higher is aft.
- `5105b9a8`'s own evidence (A3 in `aircraft-layer/research/2026-11-18-bl16-m0-flight-axes-
  findings.md`, -0.56) was a correlation against **attitude**, not acceleration, flown with SAU
  on — confounded: attitude lags the stick input, and SAU was actively holding/correcting pitch
  during that flight. The main-loop reading in `debug-2026-11-24c.md` ("Pitch direction") read an
  oscillation's phase lag the same way; also confounded.
- Counterfactual: with `LAW_DIR.pitch` reverted to `-1.0`,
  `TestPitchLawDirection::test_pitch_below_theta_d_raises_the_written_command` fails
  (`-0.37225 ≯ -0.001 + 0.001`) — reproduces the positive-feedback direction mechanically.

### Fix Applied

  `1.0` (module header, line ~174); rewrote the header comment and the trace-format comment
  (~line 2029) to state arg 1100/command 2001 positive = stick aft/nose up, citing the third
  re-fly rather than the superseded A3 reading.

  plant's fact (command 2001 positive now drives pitch **up**, not down) and the assertion

  `test_pitch_below_theta_d_raises_the_written_command`. The bit-exact take test
  (`test_take_stays_bit_exact_on_pitch`) is unaffected — `LAW_DIR` is self-inverse and cancels at
