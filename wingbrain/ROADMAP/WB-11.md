# WB-11 — Line of sight for wingbrain detection, shared or cached

- [ ] **WB-11 — A wingbrain drops what it could not see: close to the player it reuses the
  player's cached line of sight, further away it checks line of sight only for the units it would
  statistically detect, and caches the result.** #status/open Filed 2026-11-19 from
  `docs/concept/wingbrain.md`, "Wingbrain perception, detection and belief", stage 3. Needs
  `[[WB-10]]`.

  > *"Stage 3: LOS. LOS is still important. If wingman is close to player (say within 500m) simply
  > share (cached) LOS from player aircraft. If wingman if further away, do a LOS for units
  > wingbrain would statistically detect and drop those he can't see. Cache LOS for a while, so we
  > don't do it all the time."*

  The 500 m sharing radius and the cache lifetime change what a wingman sees, so they are
  tunables.
