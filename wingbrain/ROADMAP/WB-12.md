# WB-12 — Belief transfer over the radio, including Petrovich transmitting target data

- [ ] **WB-12 — A unit or group location is passed over the radio on request, and the receivers
  add it to their belief. A wingman ordered onto a target he does not know asks for target data;
  Petrovich can transmit it, on the player's ICS order, on whatever channel the pilot has
  selected.** #status/open Filed 2026-11-19 from `docs/concept/wingbrain.md`, "Wingbrain
  perception, detection and belief", stage 4, and "Radio interface". Needs `[[WB-9]]` and
  `[[WB-11]]`.

  > *"Stage 4: belief transfer between wingbrains and/or Petrovich/player. Using radio, transmit a
  > unit/group location. Receivers will add/update to belief. Only transmit on request: player
  > request to transmit or winman commanded to act on a target he does not know > asks for target
  > data > Petrovich will transmit target data."*
  >
  > *"Petrovich can transmit on radio. Use case: player command on ICS to send unit location, mark,
  > some known location or unit to flight. Petrovich then transmits that on radio (SPU-8 is pilot
  > controlled, copilot can only transmit on whatever channel is active). That transmission would
  > (as all) include text to speak so that player hears it and data section that wingbrains would
  > act on. Readback from wingbrains as normal.*
  > *- this is real use case, but a later stage thing to build"*
