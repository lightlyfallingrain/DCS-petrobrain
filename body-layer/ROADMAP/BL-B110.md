# BL-B110 — Expected-area check: tell a new sighting from a remembered group by looking where that group should be

- [ ] **BL-B110 — When a new sighting could be a group Petrovich holds only in memory, he looks at
  that group's expected area (not the whole bearing) before deciding same-or-different, and calls
  out only once the verdict is known.** #status/open

  Follow-on to `[[BL-B108]]`, which accepted a 0–86% fold rate for the memory-only cross-optic case
  and fenced it with a test. User direction 2026-11-01: *"3 but not whole bearing. Check the
  expected locations/area."* Cues: the remembered group seen at its place (→ different group);
  composition/count difference as graded suspicion; area empty → "moved" only if composition
  matches and the displacement fits plausible speed; terrain anchors only if measurably useful.
  Plan: `plans/expected-area-check/`.

  **Scope widened 2026-11-01 (goal-check Q1), architect pass:** the scan order changes to per
  sector (naked-eye sweep of a sector → binoculars on that sector's contacts → next sector),
  revising the 2026-09-23 "glass only after a full cycle" reading. It changes behaviour for every
  contact and ships as its own stage. Staged by measurement (scratch probe on `[[BL-B108]]`'s
  sweep harness): never folding an ambiguous memory-only match (Stage 2) catches most of the fold
  at a 4–10% cross-optic false-split cost; the look at the remembered area can only change what
  the pilot hears once "area seen empty" is judgeable, which needs a terrain-visibility source
  (plan Decision 2). Stages 3–4 (the look, the hold, "moved"/"gone") are conditional on that
  decision.

  **Scope trimmed 2026-11-01 (plan-check decisions, user: *"as recommended"*): BL-B110 is Stages
  0–2 (plus 2b, which only measures).**
  - Stage 0: measure and tune the trigger.
  - Stage 1: per-sector scan. Sectors come from the thirds left/ahead/right (user: *"third of full
    scan. So basically left right and ahead"*), swept ahead first and then alternating sides
    (2026-11-02: *"always start from ahead and sweep to left/right alternating"*). Today's
    `SCAN_PLAN` `(12,11,10,9,12,1,2,3)` already is that path, so it stays and gives four 4 s
    sectors. Several binocular looks per glass step, with a 12 s budget (user: *"The 12 s sounds
    ok"*) that is rolling over the last cycle and gates a look's start, never its length.
    Selectivity is the main bound: a `GLASSING` stall cooldown stops a look that taught nothing
    from being repeated at the same range. The worst flank revisit is 28 s (32 s with poll slack)
    against the 30 s position half-life. Plan Decision 6 accepts the ≤ 2 s overrun (user: *"For
    the overrun - ok."*).
    **Optics priority refined 2026-11-02 (user):** *"1 detection and rough location … 2 IFF … 3
    classification 4 exact location (for enemy units especially and mission target units or
    anything the player asks for)"*; *"Exact position is not even needed for all units."* So:
    rough location is the naked-eye scan alone (a naked-eye look is always within max(250 m,
    0.2 · R), measured; no binocular look for it); IFF is not built (`[[BL-B12]]`); a look toward
    type identification ranks before one toward a class; binoculars are raised for exact location
    only on **watched** contacts, after classification (`EXACT_LOCATION_MIN_GAIN_M`, 25 m). Plan
    Decision 8 is reversed. Checked against the user's approach profile (~100 km/h, sidestep,
    orbit): a half-field sector margin, a look that follows its contact through a turn, and a
    scenario test that flies that profile.
  - **Stage 0a (Decision 7, user: *"Option a"*), built first:** a new look never leaves the belief
    less certain than just before it, so a binocular or sight range fix survives later naked-eye
    looks (class growth still applies). It changes BL-B108's fold: BL-B108's tests are re-run
    before and after, and any moved fence stops the work. Ships with Stage 1. Binocular range sense
    stays as is (user: *"keep the current"*).
  - Stage 2: an ambiguous memory-only match is founded as a new contact and called out at once.
