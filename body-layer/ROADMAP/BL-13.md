  **The grammar** (decision 12): `report|describe [what] [where] [how far]`, every slot optional —
  his own example `"report 2 o'clock close"` has no *what*. *what* is `group` or a classification;
  *where* is typically a clock hour but also cardinal (`north`) or landmark-relative (`south of
  <village>`); *how far* is `near` < 2 km / `medium distance` 2–5 km / `far` 5 km+. The
  landmark-relative slot is **not** blocked on place names, which was an error in this entry's first
  version: `WM-B1` is `[x]` done (`fix/latin-place-names`, 2026-10-02, needing only a rebuild) and
  `query.search.find_place_by_name` already exists. The real blocker is **open-vocabulary
  recognition** — `audio-adapter`'s `vocabulary.py` has a standing rule against enumerating open
  vocabularies into a closed grammar ("belongs to free speech once the brain layer exists"), so the
  landmark slot is gated on the **brain layer**, not on a theatre rebuild. The plan lands it on the
  typed console path first (already free text) resolving into the same `sector`-shaped filter, so
  wiring it to voice later adds a parser rather than forcing a redesign; meanwhile a spoken
  `"report south of Gemerek"` degrades to a bare report rather than a `say_again`.

  **The answer shape** (decision 13), settled by putting two mock answers side by side and taking
  the user's choice: **summaries, not enumerations.** *"B. I can also look myself, so a summary is
  good, I then know where and what to look for."* The principle is that a report **aims the pilot's
  eyes**; it does not transfer the picture. So the answer's grain follows the belief's grain —
  groups, counts, a dominant classification, a bearing span, a distance band — it degrades
  gracefully under uncertainty where an enumeration would have to lie or hedge four times, and it
  costs less time to say, which matters in a hover.

  ## What shipped — Stages 1–3, 2026-10-10

  **Stage 1, the slotted token.** One `report` token in `audio-adapter`, five optional slots
  (`descriptor`/`clock`/`sector`/`relative`/`band`), parsed by `_parse_report_slots` in front of
  the phrase table and after the `follow` branch (an ordering pinned by a source-order test, not
  just by outcomes). `_REPORT_VERBS = ("report", "describe")` makes every slotted form work under
  either verb, which is what completes the `describe` synonym `0caa39e` shipped anchorable but not
  substitutable. **`BL-B28` is closed and the diagnosis in this entry's first version was
  understated**: `report left` did not fail, it resolved to **`report_bearing_e`** — *report east*
  — at 0.75, while `report right` returned nothing. Both measured on the base commit and again on
  the tip; they now return `relative: left` / `relative: right`.

  **Stage 2, the filter slots.** All five reach `_handle_report`. The clock filter widened from an
  exact hour to ±1, reusing `callouts.CALLOUT_GROUP_CLOCK_SPAN_HOURS` rather than minting a second
  constant; `relative` resolves through `perception.gaze`'s wedge table, so `report left` and
  `scan left` mean the same thing by construction; `band` filters on `relative_now["range_m"]`
  against the only two new numbers in the feature (2 000 m / 5 000 m). Q1 was answered **filter,
  not sort** (user, 2026-10-08) — a 2.1 km tank goes unmentioned to a pilot who asked about *near*.

  **Stage 3, the D13 summary answer.** `speech.render_report_summary` replaces per-group
  enumeration at the pull site with one utterance: count, dominant class, bearing span, distance
  band, at most one deferral, and — Q2, answered **yes** (user, 2026-10-08) — air defence named
  individually even when everything else aggregates.

  ### Key decisions, including where implementation diverged from the plan

  Each divergence is recorded in `plans/crew-query-path/implementation.md` and was judged by the
  Reviewer as a decision rather than drift:

  - **`PHRASES["report"]` could not carry the bare verb.** `report_all` already owns `"report"`
    and `"describe"`, and `normalized_phrase_index` *raises* on a collision — the plan's literal
    reading would not have imported. The token carries five slotted phrasings as recogniser bias
    instead, and a zero-slot `"report"` still falls through to `report_all`.
  - **`report east` / `report three o'clock` now resolve to the slotted token**, not to
    `report_bearing_e` / `report_clock_3`. A behaviour change to shipped commands, unavoidable:
    the slot branch sitting in front of the phrase table is exactly what makes `report left` work.
    The legacy tokens stay reachable from `TOKENS`, the bench corpus, body-layer dispatch,
    `brain_reply.py` and `voice_commands.py`.
  - **Six existing body-layer tests were rewritten**, because Decision C changed the command's
    output contract and no extension could preserve the old expectations. Each rewrite keeps the
    property the original existed for; two are stronger than what they replaced.
  - **The freshness marker names its own subject** (*"The long range SAM is a couple minutes
    old."*) rather than hanging off the air-defence clause, because that clause names every tier
    in scope including fresh ones, so an apposition would hedge a contact seen a second ago.

  ### Freshness: a security finding, and the user's decision that narrowed it

  The first implementation spoke freshness only when **every** in-scope contact was stale — and
  in-scope runs to `decay.LOST_THRESHOLD_S` (120 s) while the stale window is one scan cycle
  (16 s), so one contact refreshed in the current cycle stripped the qualifier off a two-minute-old
  SAM. Measured, not reasoned (`plans/crew-query-path/security.md`, required fix 1). Asked how a
  mixed-staleness report should carry it, the user chose:

  > *"Freshness is spoken only when a stale contact is air defence or otherwise decision-relevant;
  > a stale truck passes unqualified. Shortest answers, but it means accepting that some stale
  > belief is spoken as present tense."*

  So the marker is **selective and never dropped by the word budget**. *Some stale belief is
  spoken as present tense, deliberately* — that is the accepted trade, and a later reader must be
  able to tell it from a defect. *"Or otherwise decision-relevant"* is deliberately unbuilt; the
  one candidate (a *moving* contact, whose position is the fastest-decaying attribute at a 30 s
  half-life) is a question on the sortie card rather than a built taxonomy.

  ### Second-order effects on the next milestone

  **`BL-12` should still be next, and its plan should be re-read before it starts.** Three things
  bear on it:

  - **The slot schema is the brain layer's target.** When free speech arrives, the brain's job is
    filling these same five slots from open text and calling the same handler — not a new schema.
    That is why the landmark slot is parked in Stage 5 rather than approximated.
  - **`BL-B48`'s measured cohesion bridge is a BL-12 premise, not a side finding.**
    `belief.groups`' `EAGER` infantry cohesion drops the absolute backstop to `inf` for any pair
    where either member is infantry, so an infantry contact merges non-infantry contacts across
    measured 6 km. If BL-12's plan assumes today's cohesion behaviour, it is assuming a defect;
    its redesign may dissolve it, but that has to be a decision rather than a side effect.
  - **BL-13's summary is immune to that defect by construction and the push path is not.** The
    summary aggregates the filtered set and never walks a group roster; `render_group_full_
    disclosure` does. So the value of fixing group identity is now concentrated on the push path,
    which should sharpen BL-12's scope rather than widen it.
