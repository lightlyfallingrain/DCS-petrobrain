  ## What the spec adds over the 2026-09-24 conversation

  The 2026-09-24 notes designed the *guidance* half (the register ladder, the sector-keyed
  azimuth switch, the hit/damage callouts). The new spec adds the half that was missing — **how a
  target gets chosen, corrected and dropped** — and separates target selection from the attack run
  as two commands rather than one:

  - **`set|select target <what> <where>`** — selection as its own command, with an explicit
    tie-break: units matching `<what>`/`<where>`, **watched beats unwatched, then closest**, then a
    readback *"target `<what>` `<where>`"*. `<what>` is a classification *or* a type.
  - **`step [count] left|right|near|closer|close|far|further|away`** — step to the Nth next matching
    unit in that direction, **staying inside the unit group**. This is the spec's own answer to the
    selection logic picking wrong, and it is the part no earlier note anticipated.
  - **`drop target`**, plus the existing cancel/stop path also deselecting.
  - **`engage|attack|attack-run <what> <where>`** — select, then immediately start the run;
    **`engage target` / `attack run`** starts on the already-selected target.
  - **`disengage|stop attack|abort`** ends it; destruction (**fire is enough**) ends it
    automatically.

  ## What is already true, and what this changes

  - **The grammar is closed, so this does not need the brain layer.** `<what>` is a classification
    or type and `<where>` is a clock hour / sector / band — the same five slots `BL-13` Stage 1
    already parses (`descriptor`/`clock`/`sector`/`relative`/`band`), reaching the same filter path.
    The natural shape is a sixth consumer of that slot schema, not a new one. The 2026-09-24 notes
    put target resolution in the brain because the example sentence was free text (*"the tank by the
    village"*); under this spec's grammar that is the **landmark-relative slot only**, which is
    already parked behind the brain layer in `BL-13` Stage 5. Everything else resolves
    deterministically today.
  - **`BL-12` is a hard upstream dependency, not a neighbour.** *"Stay within the unit group"* is
    the whole of `step`'s semantics, and group identity is what `BL-12` exists to make stable.
    Built on today's cohesion — where `BL-B48`'s measured bridge merges non-infantry contacts
    across 6 km whenever one member is infantry — `step left` would walk out of the group the pilot
    is looking at.
  - **Group grain versus unit grain is the open design question.** Selection, `step` and
    guidance are all **per unit**, while the belief layer's grain above `BL-12` is the **group**,
    and `BL-12` establishes that per-unit identity is *not recoverable from position* at measured
    DCS spacing (16 m median against a gate inflating to 600 m). So a selected target may be a unit
    the system cannot re-identify tick to tick. This is the thing `/explore` has to settle before a
    plan exists; it is not resolvable from the code.
  - **The damage probe is answered, and it corrects one line of the spec.** The probe this feature
    was once gated on is **flown and closed** (`docs/acceptance/2026-09-24-damage-and-firing-probes.md`,
    results in `aircraft-layer/research/2026-09-24-damage-and-firing-events-over-mission-bridge.md`):
    `getLife()`/`getLife0()` work per unit and `S_EVENT_HIT` is subscribable. But the readable
    visible state is **fire, not smoke** — fire meaning doomed-with-a-delay, bracketed to a life
    fraction of (0.69, 0.84]. The spec's *"target smoking / on fire / destroyed"* therefore ships as
    **on fire / destroyed**; "smoking" has no channel. The spec's own end condition — *"on fire is
    enough"* — matches what the probe found, which is why that part needs no change.
  - **Miss detection stays deferred** (user, 2026-09-24: *"defer miss detection. Keep hit/damaged
    detection"*). So *"missed, aim higher"* is out; **"hit"**, the fire/destroyed family and
    *"target not hit, attack again?"* are in — the last needs only the absence of a hit, not an
    impact point.
  - **The advisory deferral is not being reopened.** The governing rule, from the same session:
    **advisory output is allowed when the pilot requested it and is still in the manoeuvre he
    requested it for; unrequested advisory is deferred.** `engage` is the request; `disengage` and
    destruction are the end conditions. *"Break right"* on Petrovich's own initiative remains
    deferred.
  - **The register switch needs no tuned constant.** Settled 2026-09-24: inside the 12 o'clock
    sector (±15° of the nose) azimuth is spoken as signed degrees, outside it as the o'clock;
    range switches km→m below 1 km, independently. `OwnshipState` already carries `pitch_deg` and
    `bank_deg` with both sign conventions user-confirmed, so *"15 degrees down"* costs geometry and
    phrasing, not a new data channel.
  - **`lined up` means azimuth-aligned, not will-hit.** The user: *"with rockets/gun attack run is
    almost never exactly boresight, it has to take trajectory into account, that's pilots job."*
    Ballistics stay with the pilot, deliberately.
  - **Callout rate is the performance risk.** Guidance is *frequent* callouts on a single contact
    while `BL-11` has only just made tick cost visible and `BL-B49` has `_report_scope` enriching
    every contact before any filter runs (63.25 ms/contact). A high-rate single-target path should
    not reuse a whole-scene enrichment walk.

  ## 2026-10-13 update — the grain question is reframed, not yet resolved

  The `/explore` above ran, ahead of its own stated sequencing (triggered by this question
  directly rather than waiting for a separate pass), and produced
  `docs/acceptance/2026-10-13-group-model-and-attention-tiers.md`. **Verbatim, the answer
  reframes the unit-versus-group grain question rather than settling it either way**: resolution
  depends on attention tier, not a single global policy. At low/medium tier the group fold's
  undercount is correct behaviour (noise reduction is the goal); at high tier (watched, targeted,
  or directly asked about) the evidence to resolve individuals exists and should be used. So
  `BL-14` needs **per-unit records for selected targets specifically**, produced by an
  attention-driven resolution mechanism that does not exist yet — not a blanket per-unit belief
  layer and not today's unconditional fold either.

  **`collapse(group, depth)` (`plans/group-collapse/plan.md`, merged) is a step toward the
  reporting half of this, not the selection half.** It gives a cheap, derived, depth-bounded view
  of a group's membership for the *pull* path (one group, on demand) — exactly `BL-14`'s own
  per-target reporting shape — but it delivers a projection of the group's *existing* members,
  never a record resolved to a unit the fold would otherwise have absorbed. The two of six
  real-unit gap `BL-12`'s own measurement found (units with no record at all) is untouched by
  `collapse`; its plan says so directly (Decision 3, finding B). **`BL-14` still needs the
  attention-driven resolution mechanism**, either as its own scope or a milestone sequenced ahead
  of it — `collapse` narrows what that mechanism has to do (report through an existing derivation
  rather than invent a second roster shape) without supplying it.

  ## 2026-10-14 update — `/explore` run, direction captured, plan written

  The `/explore` ran and produced
  `docs/acceptance/2026-10-14-attack-run-guidance-direction.md`, **which is now the specification**
  — it reverses the guidance *register* of both `docs/concept/Attacking target.md` and the
  2026-09-24 notes (no "N degrees down", no range countdown, qualitative words not numbers, silence
  means aligned, sector-based register switch, change-driven cadence), while their *grammar* and
  the two-loop reasoning stand. It also **reverses the 2026-10-13 Part 1 finding** that `step right`
  must walk the world: stepping walks the **belief store**, ordered angularly around the current LOS
  and recomputed at each press, so `BL-14` is **no longer gated on the unbuilt attention-driven
  per-unit resolution mechanism** for its stepping half. The undercount (4 records / 6 real units)
  remains, as an accepted honest limit on what `step` can reach.

  Plan: `plans/attack-run/plan.md` (Architect, 2026-10-14; **amended the same day** after direction
  §15–§18 landed) — **7 stages, 2 open decisions** for the user; all three original open decisions
  are now settled by §15/§16/§17.

  **The amendment's consequential change is §18: guidance consumes a new perception datum, an
  ownship-relative observation, rather than world-anchored `PositionBelief`.** *"Where is it on the
  map"* and *"where is it relative to me right now"* are two different questions with two different
  error models; `BEARING_SIGMA_DEG` models *stating* a bearing and is the wrong model for *"come
  left"* while looking at the target. That added a stage (the channel, inert), moved the gaze claim
  into the wiring stage (tier 1 only holds while the target is looked at), and gave the §7 fallback
  its own stage. The general version of the channel is filed as `BL-B62`, deliberately **not** in
  `BL-14`'s scope.

  Two findings there are worth seeing from here: `Contact.last_alt_m` is the **ownship's** own
  altitude at the last look, not the target's ground elevation — a live trap, though §17 then
  removed the world-model elevation query the plan first proposed as its remedy (*"No world
  elevation necessary since unit data has elevation"* — per-object `altitude_m` is on the wire and
  is simply dropped at clustering); and raising a selected target's attention to `"priority"` would
  reintroduce the struck range countdown through `_WATCHED_ONLY_KINDS`, which the plan closes with
  one narrow gate. Damage reporting and the automatic stop on destruction are a **stated gap**
  against the spec (§13) — a run ends on pilot command only.

  **`BL-B61` also bears on `step left|right`'s "stay inside the unit group" semantics**: at class
  level, `group_matches` test 4 cannot distinguish an armoured company from a SAM site (both
  resolve to `OP_GROUPSOMETHING` through `object_model.profile_for`), so group identity itself is
  weaker than `step`'s spec assumes it to be. Filed, not fixed — see that entry.

  `RELATIVE_DIRECTION_SIGMA_DEG = 0.0`), not from world-anchored belief, because "where is it on

  models (§18 of the direction document). The register switch is sector-based (±15° about the
  nose), not range-based. Cadence is change-driven, not ticked. Three constants
  (`ATTACK_RUN_LINED_UP_AZIMUTH_DEG=5.0`, `ATTACK_RUN_ELEVATION_GATE_DEG=10.0`,
  `ATTACK_RUN_CHANGE_THRESHOLD_DEG=3.0`) are deliberately unset agreed guesses, to be tuned from
  flying. Group-as-target was dropped, not deferred — the pilot approximates it by selecting one
  unit. Damage/destroyed reporting and the automatic stop on destruction are a **known, stated gap**
  against the user's own spec (§13) — not built, because the damage channel is probe-answered but
  unwired; a run ends only on pilot command.
