  and what he can do follows from that seat and from whether he is flying.** #status/open Filed
  2026-11-17 at user direction, priority 3 in `[[X-T29]]`. Verbatim:

  > *"pilot/copilot difference*
  > *- Player can choose between pilot and copilot seat. Petrovich is in the \*other\* seat.*

  So:

  - **Player in the copilot seat → Petrovich is the pilot.** He has naked eye and binoculars only;
    the 9K113 belongs to the copilot (operator) seat, so it is the player's, not his.

  - **While Petrovich flies, in either `[[BL-16]]` mode, he uses no optic at all.** His hands are on
    the controls. This is the hands rule of `[[BL-B116]]` (eyes, hands, mouth as explicit
    resources), applied to flying.
