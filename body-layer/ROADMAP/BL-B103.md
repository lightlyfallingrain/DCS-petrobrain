  User direction, 2026-10-29, verbatim, arising from `[[BL-B67]]`'s plan question N3 (the ED list
  is not bounded by where our Petrovich is looking):

  > *"DCS AI list was an early approach to using DCS AI to use the sight for us and what it could
  > detect. Now that we are building our own model, we can completely stop using the DCS AI overlay
  > list. We are moving towards complete (as much as possible) replacing DCS AI Petrovich. We can
  > simply drop reading that list."*

  **What it is.** `perception/hybrid_source.py`'s `HybridPerceptionSource` gates on ED's AI Helper
  overlay text (`GET /petrovich_indication/latest`, `aircraft-layer/research/2026-09-08-pb1-live-spike-results.md`)
  and takes geometry from `LoGetWorldObjects`. ED's Petrovich runs its own search, which nothing ties
  to our Petrovich's gaze or optic FOV, so a contact can enter belief that he could not have seen —
  against the no-omniscience principle. It is the original detection channel, built before the
  naked-eye/sight model existed.

  **Scope is wider than one file.** As of 2026-10-29 the source tag
  `petrovich_detection_associated` and the indication client are referenced across `body.py`,
  `belief/contacts.py`, `association_over_time.py`, `percept.py`, `speech.py`, `callouts.py`,
  `crew_console.py`, `enrichment.py`, `tasks.py`, `perception/association.py`/`source.py`/
  `naked_eye_source.py`, and aircraft-layer's indication/wheel schema, cache and API (plus the BL-6
  `petrovich_search` wheel command). Which of those go and which are shared with the naked-eye path
  is the plan's to work out; `grep` the source tag and the client method first.

  **Not part of `[[BL-B67]]`.** The ATGM run already holds ED-list sightings during a missile's
  flight; this removes the channel everywhere. Related: `[[BL-1]]` (where the hybrid source was
  built), `[[BL-B67]]`, `project_ed_petrovich_failures` (auto-memory: long-term goal is full
  replacement of ED's Petrovich).
