- [ ] **BL-B136 — An explosion makes its position a presence-only light source at night, like a
  launch flash or a burning wreck.** #status/open Split out of `[[BL-B124]]` 2026-11-25 (user,
  U1: *"as recommended"* — defer explosions to their own item, build the rest of BL-B124 now).

  The user listed explosions among the night light sources (`plans/bl-b124-light-level/
  explore-notes.md`). Unlike gun firing, burning and missile/rocket launches, DCS gives us no
  position for them today: the firing-events Hook (`aircraft-layer/dcs-export/
  petrobrain-firing-events-hook.lua`) does not forward `S_EVENT_HIT`, and none of the events it
  does forward except `S_EVENT_SHOT` carries a resolved position
  (`aircraft-layer/research/2026-11-25-mission-start-time-and-light-level-export-api.md`). The work
  is a Hook diff (new event kind plus `pcall`-guarded position resolution, the same mechanism
  `S_EVENT_SHOT` already uses), a collector branch, and an `EmitterKind.EXPLOSION` folded into
  `resolve_emitters()` beside `LAUNCH_FLASH` — BL-B124's plan Stage 6 has the outline.
