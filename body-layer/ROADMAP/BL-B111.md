# BL-B111 — Expected-area look: point line-of-sight query, then look where a remembered group should be

- [ ] **BL-B111 — Petrovich looks at a remembered group's expected area before deciding whether a
  new sighting on its bearing is the same group. A point line-of-sight query through the LOS Hook
  tells him whether that place was visible at all. He then says "now at …" or that the group is no
  longer there, instead of always calling the sighting new.** #status/open

  Depends on `[[BL-B110]]`, and is its second half. It was split out on 2026-11-01 by the
  plan-check decisions (user: *"as recommended"*): BL-B110 ships Stages 0–2, which never fold an
  ambiguous match and always call B out as new. This entry is that plan's Stages 3–4, **opened now
  so that a quiet flight cannot quietly cancel it.** The flight count of duplicate contacts
  (`sortie-log-triage`, gate-path foundings near a remembered contact) sets its priority, not
  whether it is built.

  **What it adds for the pilot:**
  - The 4–10% cross-optic false split that BL-B110 accepts (one group heard as two) is removed by a
    "same" verdict.
  - When A's area is seen empty, the pilot hears the relocation line or "no longer at its last
    place".
  - Every verdict rests on something Petrovich saw.

  **Design:** `plans/expected-area-check/plan.md`, section "Stages 3–4 — moved to BL-B111". It
  covers:
  - Stage 3: when "seen empty" is judgeable. Visibility source **option (a)**: the Hook takes
    probe points and returns `land.isVisible` terrain-clear for each. It fails closed, so no
    answer means "cannot look", never "empty".
  - Stage 4: the check state, the actions and the one "when" function; the hold, bounded per
    sector (≈11 s with the three-sector scan); the verdict table (Q2 threat-first and Q5 ATGM
    pause included); composition and displacement; the contact-removal co-moving set.

  **Wording, user decision 2026-11-01:** *"~"A now at...""*. The relocation line is neutral,
  ≈ *"That group, now two o'clock, three kilometres."*, never "has moved". Petrovich cannot tell a
  move from a misjudged range. Explore the exact template together with `[[BL-B109]]`.

  **Before planning:** a goal check, then an architect pass that re-reads the moved stages against
  what BL-B110 actually shipped: the Stage 0 trigger choice, the final `GLASS_STEP_MAX_S` and the
  measured residuals. Touches aircraft-layer (Lua Hook, schema, collector) and needs a Windows
  verification.
