# BL-B104 — Ground contacts are on the ground: position from perceived direction, not a guessed range

- [ ] **BL-B104 — A ground unit's believed position comes from the perceived direction (bearing
  and depression, both with angular error) meeting the unit's ground height (AGL 0), not from a
  perturbed range guess at the observer's own altitude.** #status/open

  **Bug, found 2026-10-30** (`docs/acceptance/2026-10-30-sortie-feedback.md` §4). The sight aim
  during an engaged run was nowhere near the target, and the sight never acquired it. Two log-traced
  causes, both in how a contact's position is believed:

  - **Altitude.** `perception.geometry.project_from_bearing_range` puts a percept at the
    *observer's* altitude, and `Contact.last_alt_m` keeps that. Run 1: the target was seen once
    at 1584 m ownship altitude; after a 419 m descent the believed point was above the aircraft
    and the believed elevation climbed to +72 deg (truth was -84 deg on the overflight). A partial
    fix for the sight path only (`body._look_target_position` reusing
    `attack_run.remembered_target_alt_m`) is on branch `fix/atgm-run-sight-tracking`.
  - **Range.** A naked-eye/binocular percept carries a perturbed *range* estimate. Run 2: looks at
    4.7-5.7 km put the believed point on the correct bearing but ~700 m short. A wrong range is a
    wrong depression angle, and that error grows as the aircraft closes. The aim swung away from
    the real group.

  **The user's rule, general to all detection and belief** (2026-10-30, verbatim):

  > *"while we don't want to break the no-omniscience rule and tell Petrovoch exactly where any
  > unit is, including altitude, ground units are *on the ground*. We may not be certain of the
  > exact ground elevation at that point, but we know for certain that the unit is at AGL 0. Any
  > unit at AGL > 0 is aircraft or weapon in the air."*
  >
  > *"this rule is general one about unit detection and belief. Ground units are *on the ground*."*
  >
  > *"The likely elevation error would be in the same range as the azimuth angular error. The
  > closer the target is, the more we look at it, the better we can tell the exact location,
  > including (ground) elevation (at unit location). Technically that elevation comes from A) unit
  > data B) we can query terrain elevation at that point from DCS, which allows separation of
  > ground unit and air unit"*
  >
  > *"no world model elevation grid"*

  **Agreed shape** (proposed and accepted, *"This sounds good"*, 2026-10-30):

  ```
  perceived:      bearing ± σ  and  depression ± σ   (similar angular error; shrinks closer / with more looks)
  not guessed:    range
  ground unit:    position = perceived direction ray ∩ the unit's ground height (AGL 0)
  air unit:       unchanged (today's range estimate)
  ```

  - **Ground height: option A, unit data** — the ground unit's own DCS altitude, which is the
    terrain height where it stands. One computation per percept, no new query. Option B (live DCS
    `land.getHeight` via aircraft-layer) only if A proves insufficient.
  - **Ground vs air: DCS unit category** (unit data; matches what an observer sees). B's AGL test
    is the fallback if the category is unreliable.
  - **No world-model elevation grid** (user, above; consistent with the 2026-10-05 direction that
    world-model terrain is testing-only on the live path).
  - **No-omniscience fence holds**: what reaches belief is still only a noisy direction; the unit's
    ground height places that direction on the ground. Angular error must stay the percept's own,
    per optic and range, as the existing estimation model tunes it.

  **Scope.** Perception (`naked_eye_source`, `hybrid_source`, `estimation`,
  `perception.geometry`), contact memory (`Contact.last_position`/`last_alt_m`, fusion/association
  gating, which today depends on the flat projection's exact behaviour: `project_from_bearing_range`
  docstring), and every consumer of a contact's position: reports, guidance words
  (`attack_run.remembered_target_alt_m` becomes unnecessary for ground contacts), sight aim. Needs a
  plan (Architect) before building; consequential.

  **Relation to `fix/atgm-run-sight-tracking`.** That branch carries (1) the sight-path altitude
  workaround above, superseded by this item for ground contacts, and (2) the decided
  bearing-line depression search plus the "Tracking." callout (user: *"do search as you
  recommended. Say "tracking" when on target"*), which stays as the fallback for far,
  shallow-angle targets where a small depression error is still a large range error.
