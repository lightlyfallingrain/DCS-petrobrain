  ## Settled in the explore (2026-10-25)

  1. **Petrovich is the copilot for now.** The controller is built seat-agnostic, so pilot-Petrovich
     can follow.
  2. **Handover is spoken only.** "You have controls" → *"I have controls"*, and the reverse.
     Petrovich's optics stop while he flies and come back when he hands over. **Physical stick input
     is ignored while he has the controls:** *"I cannot disable my joystick in flight … if I
     unintentionally move it, it must be ignored."*
  3. **On takeover he maintains the current state, and that includes a turn already in progress:**
     *"keep the turn going, that is what I mean by maintaining current state"*. He takes the axes
     over from where the player left them, with no jump.
  4. **The SAU holds the steady state. The raw-axis closed loop flies the manoeuvres.**
  5. **The control response follows S-curves with no oscillation** (page 3's drawing: a smooth
     approach, never overshoot and ringing).
  6. **First vocabulary:** *"turn [x deg] left/right"*, *"turn heading 3 1 5"*, *"hold 200 m
     [radar/barometric]"*, *"speed 2 2 0"*.
  7. **Whoever flies avoids the terrain.** Petrovich does it autonomously from current AGL. The
     player still steers him clear of terrain and buildings through commands, because DCS data has
     limits. Avoidance from the elevation along the flight path is split out as `[[BL-B101]]`.
  8. **Manoeuvres such as "break left" are later.** They are built on the scripting surface of
     `[[BL-B95]]`, which page 3 asks to expose the params and actions to, and they fall under root
     `CLAUDE.md`, "Behaviour as actions and params".

  ## Before any plan: measure (stage 0, investigator plus a Windows probe)

  - Can attitude, IAS, vertical speed, radar altitude and the stick/collective positions be read per
    frame?

  **What the SAU holds, per the user (2026-10-26):** pitch/bank/yaw well, course in course mode,
  hover once established; **altitude poorly, and speed/altitude possibly not implemented** (an older
  DCS document). So `hold 200 m` and `speed 2 2 0` need Petrovich's own loop rather than
  the SAU — confirm in stage 0. **That loop flies the lift vector** (user direction, 2026-10-26):
  desired acceleration sets the vector, cyclic its direction, collective its length — never
  "collective for altitude, cyclic for speed". Right-yaw pedal margin is small at low speed. **Feedback, not a model**
  (same day): corrections from goal-minus-current-state and the current trend, applied
  incrementally and smoothly — no absolute lift vector is computed. Verbatim: `plans/flight-control/explore-notes.md`, addendum 2026-10-26.

  ## Shape, as far as it is known

  The control loop runs per frame inside Export.lua (aircraft-layer), the same pattern as the 9K113
  slew loop. Body-layer sends targets (heading, turn rate, altitude and its reference, speed) over
  the existing HTTP seam and owns the handover state, the vocabulary and the terrain floor. Running
  the controller across the LAN would bring back the oscillation page 3 rules out.
