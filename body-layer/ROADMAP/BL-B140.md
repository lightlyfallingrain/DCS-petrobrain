# BL-B140 — Mode 2 "speed 250" resolves to MAX instead of the 250 step

#status/in-progress · #needs-flight · branch `fix/mode2-recovery-wake`

User, 2026-11-27, verbatim: *"command "speed 2 5 0" gets me 'speed max', though it should get
'speed 250'. There is 250 speed option in AI overlay."*

Cause: `nearest_speed_step` (`body-layer/src/belief/ai_pilot_wheel.py`) used `>=` against the top
real step (250), so exactly 250 snapped to the MAX sentinel; its own docstring and the Lua goal tick
(`target_kmh > AI_PILOT_SPEED_TOP_STEP_KMH`) both say "above". A test asserted the wrong value.
Fix: `>`; 250 → 250, above 250 → MAX. Side effect, also correct: "speed down" from 250 now steps to
200 (it used to resolve 250 as MAX and step down to 250, a no-op).
