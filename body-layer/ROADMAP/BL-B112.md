# BL-B112 — Motion-aware contact certainty: a stationary unit stays tracked

- [~] **BL-B112 — Tracked → estimated → lost takes the contact's movement into account. A unit last
  seen stationary (or entrenched) stays "tracked" well past 30 s unseen, and "lost" depends on how
  far it could plausibly have moved, not on a fixed 120 s.** #status/in-progress (`feature/motion-aware-certainty`)

  Queued after `[[BL-B110]]` (user, 2026-11-02: *"own item, queue after this"*).

  User's words (2026-11-02, `plans/expected-area-check/explore-notes.md`):

  > *"Tracking vs estimated. Again, the unit movement should be considered. Stationary unit can be
  > expected to remain stationary. Therefore even if no one looked at it for 30s it's not likely to
  > have moved."*

  **Today:** `belief.decay.certainty_of` is time-only — observed ≤ `OBSERVED_WINDOW_S`, tracked ≤
  `POSITION_HALF_LIFE_S` (30 s), estimated ≤ `LOST_THRESHOLD_S` (120 s), then lost — for every
  contact alike. Position uncertainty already grows per class (`position_belief.
  plausible_speed_mps`, 0 for an entrenched installation), but the certainty label does not use it.
  The label drives the "Last I saw" wording, lifecycle events (lost/reacquired),
  `position_confidence` and every `observed`/`fresh` filter in `belief/tools.py` and
  `belief/contacts.py`.

  **For the pilot:** a SAM site or parked column he was told about does not turn into "last I saw
  it" after half a minute of Petrovich looking elsewhere, and does not go "lost" while it is
  certainly still there. With missions of a handful of groups a few km apart, approached slowly and
  often circled at a distance, most known groups spend most of the time unseen for 30 s or more.

  **Open, for Explore/goal check:** what counts as "seen stationary" (motion belief, class, or
  both); whether a stationary unit can ever become "estimated"/"lost" by time alone; the coupled
  invariants in `tuning._check_scan_decay_coupling` and `GROUP_REIDENTIFY_WINDOW_S`, which are
  derived from `POSITION_HALF_LIFE_S`. Also makes `[[BL-B110]]`'s accepted ≤ 2 s overrun of the
  half-life moot for stationary contacts.
