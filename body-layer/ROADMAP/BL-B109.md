  callout that sounds like a repeat.** #status/open

  good idea"*, `plans/relative-distance-grouping/explore-notes.md`), and the wording is
  deliberately **not** in `[[BL-B108]]`'s scope.

  Worth building once `[[BL-B108]]` reliably produces two contacts for two groups on one bearing.
  Before that, the case it words rarely exists. Needs an `/explore` pass on the wording, plus a
  goal check: which group is "first", and what "beyond" or "short of" means when the relative
  judgement is itself uncertain.
