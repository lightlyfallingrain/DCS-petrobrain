  ## What to conclude

  - **Roughly flat** — the gate works. Close this, and lever D (below) stays unnecessary.
  - **Growing at the old rate** — the gate is being read and ignored, which is information about
    advisory hooks in general, not just this one. Then lever D is the next move.
  - **Shrinking** — check *what* went, against the never-delete markers: a date, a verbatim user
    quote, a measurement with its source.

  ## Lever D, held pending this measurement

  **Cut rules a hook already reprints, not only their evidence.** The 2026-10-09 pass moved evidence
  out and kept every rule; where `agent-sha-gate.sh` or `agent-worktree-reminder.sh` reprints the
  rule itself at dispatch, the copy in `AGENTS.md` is a duplicate — and duplication is the rot
  mechanism this whole series is about.

  **Flagged as the risky one, and the risk is not symmetrical.** A rule that is cut and then
  violated does not announce the connection; the violation simply looks like a mistake. Do not start
  this without the user, and do not start it on an unmeasured gate.
