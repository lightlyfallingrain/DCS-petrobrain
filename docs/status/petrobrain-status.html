Waiting on you, not on code

Down from three — the Windows-box work is done and passed. What is left needs a real flight, and the voice test carries the accumulated debt along with it.

  • Sortie
    Hear it in the air

    Whether 0.6–0.8 s of synthesis latency reads as crew-like or laggy, whether callouts queue acceptably under load, and whether a generic voice is tolerable in a loud cockpit. No test can answer any of it.

  • Debt
    Accumulated live-acceptance debt

    BL-4's attention tools may never have had a live run; the F10 vocabulary is half-cleared. Both clear on the same flight as the voice test.

Open work

Milestone-level only. The reasoning behind each — why a constant moved, what an earlier pass got wrong — stays in the roadmap files, which remain the source of truth.

body-layer
Stage 4b — say the counts out loud

Five stages of group-contact work are invisible outside the console until this lands. Adds the count clause to speech and a cardinality-changed event. Singular output must stay byte-identical — that is the regression guard for every existing speech test.

Next
srs-adapter
Slice 2 — SRS intercom injection

Confirmed reachable: stock SRS declares an Intercom radio for the Mi-24P at 100.0 MHz. ICS is the only acceptable target — the SPU-8 selects one source at a time, so a dedicated frequency would compete with mission comms. It also makes Petrovich's volume adjustable from the cockpit, which local playback can never do.

Open
srs-adapter
Slice 3 — speech in, gated by a joystick PTT

Inbound bypasses SRS entirely and captures the microphone directly, because SRS offers no clean way to get the player's voice back out. Outbound always open, inbound explicitly gated — deliberately asymmetric.

Open
body-layer
Contact report fine tuning

A running list from flying with TTS live. Spell out "m" and "km"; space or expand acronyms (SAM is the exception TTS already says correctly); "on the road" at zero distance. One item needs world-model support: "200 metres north of the road" requires a bearing describe_position does not return.

Open
cross-cutting
One command to start the services

Four long-running processes across two machines, each launched by hand. Docker covers at most three of five — winsound and macOS say/afplay are exactly the host facilities two services exist to use.

Open
world-model
Multi-theatre support

Architecture already generalises through per-theatre registries. Kola is the real problem: SRTM has no coverage above 60°N, so the elevation source must change.

body-layer
Stage 5 — composition

"Three of them tanks… and something else." Held deliberately: the angular model resolved the twelve-unit case that motivated the whole group model, so the headline example largely dissolved. Re-judge after flying, not before.

Re-judge
srs-adapter
Voice character — accent and prosody

Two problems, not one. No Russian-accented English voice exists in macOS say, and the delivery is monotonous regardless of whether the line is a routine report or "break right". Prosody may matter more than accent.
