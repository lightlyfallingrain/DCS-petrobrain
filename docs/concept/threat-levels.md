# Threat levels and how they should influence contact reporting

| type | threat priority | 
| --- | --- | 
| missile, gunfire, etc. aimed at self | urgent | 
| missile, gunfire, etc. aimed at flight | urgent | 
| hostile enemy unit, dangerous to self or flight (air defense, aircraft) | urgent | 
| enemy unit dangerous to flight or mission friendlies (if escorting or providing support to friendly units) | high | 
| enemy unit, not dangerous to self/flight | medium | 
| enemy air defense, not tracking self/flight | high |
| mission target | high |
| enemy unit, far, not on flight path, not capable of firing at us | low |
| friendly unit, mission important (e.g. mission escort target, flight to follow) | high |
| friendly unit in / near target area | high |
| friendly unit in enemy territory | high |
| friendly unit near frontline | medium |
| friendly unit near target we/flight are engaging | high |
| friendly unit in friendly terrain | low | 
| friendly unit in deep friendly terrain | ignore |
| friendly unit near/at friendly airport/FAB | ignore |
| friendly unit near/at friendly airport/FAB, imminent collision risk | urgent |
| unknown unit | medium |
| neutral unit | low |

High threat priority units must be prioritized in contact reporting. Urgent level units must receive automatic 'tracking' status, until their threat level decreases.
Ignore level units do not need reporting. I know friendly bases are full of friendly units, drop noise.
Low priority units can get a slower and less granular response, like "friendly ground 10 o'clock", "friendly helicopter 3 o'clock"

Clarification on what units are dangerous to self (or flight or escort target)
- capable of firing at us (weapon type, range, engagement envelope)
- tracking us
    - LOS consideration
- flying intercept course to us
Not all need to be true to pose danger.

Not dangerous target:
- unit well outside it's engagement envelope
- unit does not have capable weapon to fire at us
- unit not capable of tracking us (LOS)
    - exception: unit was actively tracking us, lost track, now no LOS -> still urgent if LOS reaqcuired
"us" can also refer to mission escort target, support target, be it aircraft or ground units.
