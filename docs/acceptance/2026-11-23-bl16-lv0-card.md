# BL-16 lift-vector law, Stage LV-0 — test card (2026-11-23)

**Branch to fly: `feature/bl16-lift-vector-law`**.

In the repo root: `git checkout feature/bl16-lift-vector-law`, then your usual redeploy and
restarts. **The redeploy is unchanged**: your existing script that copies `Export.lua` and the
whole `Petrobrain/` folder already picks up the new `flight_profile.lua` file — no new deploy step.
Back to `main` afterwards with `git checkout main`.

SAU H, K and T **off** before every take (U4) — this stage does not use or depend on the SAU.

## What this checks

LV-0 is **instruments and shadow — no behaviour change**. Mode 1 flies exactly as it did before
this stage; nothing new is wired into the control law. What's new:

- A per-aircraft profile file (`flight_profile.lua`) that every existing loop gain now reads from
  — confirmed bit-identical in the automated test suite, this card is the live confirmation.
- An **estimator** that runs every frame, in every mode (including while the player is flying, no
  take ever requested), computing velocity/acceleration/ground-speed/Euler-rates/slip-ball from raw
  telemetry at 30 Hz (model time, never wall clock) and publishing them on the status line
  (`fc_est_*`) — this card is almost entirely about watching those numbers against the real
  aircraft and the cockpit instruments.
- A flag-gated per-frame trace (`aircraft_layer_flight_debug.flag` → `aircraft_layer_flight_trace.log`,
  same mechanism as the existing `aircraft_layer_debug.flag`) for the rows below that need finer
  detail than the 5 Hz status line gives.

## Setup

Air start, straight and level, safe height (300–800 m AGL unless a row says otherwise). Collector
address from `run-scripts/petrobrain.env`:

```sh
source run-scripts/petrobrain.env
while true; do curl -s http://$DCS_COLLECTOR_IP:7791/flight_control/latest | python3 -c "import sys,json; d=json.load(sys.stdin) or {}; print(d.get('t'), d.get('fc_mode'), d.get('fc_est_a_f'), d.get('fc_est_a_r'), d.get('fc_est_a_u'), d.get('fc_est_v_g'), d.get('fc_est_v_g_f'), d.get('fc_est_phi_dot'), d.get('fc_est_theta_dot'), d.get('fc_est_psi_dot'), d.get('fc_est_ball'), d.get('fc_est_lambda'))"; sleep 0.5; done
```

Columns: sim time, mode, `a_f`/`a_r`/`a_u` (heading-frame acceleration, m/s², `a_u` should read
near zero in level flight regardless of bank), ground speed/ground speed along nose (m/s),
Euler `phi_dot`/`theta_dot`/`psi_dot` (deg/s), the averaged ball, the first-cut `λ` curve (ignore
— not used by anything yet).

**Trace on, for rows (a)/(c)/(e)/(f)**: touch `Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\aircraft_layer_flight_debug.flag`
(empty file, any content) before the flight; delete it afterwards. Read
`Saved Games\DCS\Logs\aircraft_layer_flight_trace.log` after landing for those rows.

## Steps

**(a) Take retest on Stage 3 alone, trace on.** Voice take ("you have controls") and an unpark
(m2 → m1) with a known desired state set first. This is **not expected to be fixed by this stage**
(the law hasn't changed) — it's a retest so a later LV-1 flight has one cause to chase, not two
(alignment finding 4). Watch for the same erratic-take symptom the 2026-11-20 sortie saw. Note
whether it reproduces, and if so, how (same shape, different, gone).

**(b) Level 60 s northbound, then 60 s eastbound.** Steady altitude, steady heading, no turns.
Compare `v_g` (speed column) and the *direction* implied by `a_f`/`a_r` staying near zero against
the compass heading: northbound, `v_g,f` should read essentially all of `v_g` with `a_f`/`a_r`
near zero (no acceleration in steady flight); eastbound the same, on the new heading. This is the
position-axis check (x north, y up, z east) — if it looks backwards or rotated 90°, say so exactly.

**(c) Steady 30° and 60° turns, both directions, trace on.** Hold a steady coordinated turn for at
least 10 s at each bank angle, both left and right. From the trace, compare the body pitch rate
`q` (not logged directly on the status line — this is in `aircraft_layer_debug.log` if the general
debug flag is also on, or note the ADI/VSI behaviour) against `fc_est_theta_dot` (Euler pitch
rate) — the plan's own claim is `q ≈ ψ̇·sin(φ)` (nonzero, rising with bank) while
`theta_dot ≈ 0` (level pitch attitude, not actually changing). Also watch `fc_est_a_u` through each
turn — it should stay near zero throughout (level flight, no vertical acceleration), unlike a body-
frame G-meter reading, which would read roughly 2 g at 60° bank.

**(d) Level acceleration and deceleration.** From steady level flight, push the nose over briefly
to accelerate, then back to decelerate. Watch `fc_est_a_f`'s sign: positive while accelerating
forward, negative while decelerating. Note the sign explicitly (this sets up LV-1's trust in the
acceleration channel).

**(e) `LoGetVectorVelocity` vs `dP/dt`, all three axes, including a sideslip row. Trace on.**
Fly the northbound/eastbound legs again (or reuse (b) if the trace was already on), then add one
sideslip row: steady level flight, feed in a step of pedal to induce sustained sideslip while
holding heading and altitude (do not fully coordinate the turn — the point is lateral motion with
no turn). From the trace log, compare `vec_n`/`vec_u`/`vec_e` (the logged cross-check columns)
against `v_n`/`v_u`/`v_e` (the selected source, currently `"position"`) on all three axes,
including during the sideslip row. **This sets `VELOCITY_SOURCE`** (currently `"position"` in
`flight_profile.lua`) — report whether the two sources agree (axes and sign) closely enough to
switch to `"vector"` for LV-1, or whether a mismatch on any axis should keep `"position"`.

**(f) 10 s straight and level, trace on, for the vibration/noise floor.** No control inputs. From
the trace log, eyeball the spread in `a_f`/`a_r`/`a_u` over the 10 s — the plan's own pass bar is
"the noise of `a` in steady flight is small against 0.5 m/s²". Note the rough peak-to-peak spread
you see.

**(g) Frame-time watch (perf, background check throughout).** No dedicated manoeuvre — just note,
across the whole flight, whether DCS feels any less smooth than normal (stutter, frame-time spikes)
with the estimator now running every frame in every mode, including the whole time before any take
is requested. This is the perf-plan-review's own MONITOR item; a clean "no, felt the same" is a
real and useful answer.
