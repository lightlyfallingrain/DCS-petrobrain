Speech Word Fixes

Checkout git checkout main && git pull

Six fixes to the flight voice commands, found by bench-testing the real recognizer against recordings of your own voice. Say each phrase below exactly as written and check it against the expected result.

What "verified" means on this card: every phrase below was run through the real matching code with this exact text — not spoken, not through a microphone, not through whisper. What that proves: the logic is correct for this text. What it does not prove: that saying it out loud produces this text. That part is yours to find out.
1
text-verified
Sayaltitude three hundred
Expectholds 300 m, reads back "300 meters"
Record — did he hold 300, or say "say again"?
2
text-verified
Sayhold altitude
Expectholds current altitude, "Holding N meters"
This is the exact defect D1 fixed — "hold alt" used to get heard as "hold out" 2 times in 4 on the bench.
Record — held, or misheard again?
3
text-verified
Sayturn left three zero
Expect30° left turn, "30 left"
Not "turn left thirty" — digit words only. See the digits box below.
Record — 30° left?
4
text-verified
Sayturn heading two seven zero
Expectturns to heading 270, "Heading 270"
The D6 fix — the real shipped rule used to silently require "heading" twice ("turn heading heading two seven zero"). This exact phrase failed before today.
Record — turned to 270, or "say again"?
5
text-verified
Sayturn heading to seven zero
Expectsame as above — heading 270
Deliberately say "to" instead of "two" — the D3 homophone fix, for when whisper hears "to" where you said "two".
Record — "to" resolved to 270, or rejected?
6
text-verified
Sayright
Expecttranslates right, "Right." (operator seat, AI hover active)
7
text-verified
Sayalright
Expectsame as #6 — translates right, "Right."
The D4 fix — before it, "alright" was the one wrong-command case on the bench: it fired the legacy point command instead.
Record — same as "right", or did it do something else (point)?
8
text-verified
Sayright four five
Expect"say again" — never a silent command
The D5 fix. Before it, a dropped "turn" (a real whisper failure, not hypothetical) made this silently fire point with no sign anything went wrong.
Record — "say again", or did something silently happen? (worth checking belief/attention state)
9
text-verified
Sayheading two seven zero
Expectnot a turn — falls through to the legacy bearing-report path
No "turn"/"fly" in front — the verb is required now (D6).
Record — did the aircraft actually turn (wrong) or just report/do nothing flight-wise (right)?

Numbers — say digits, not words

"Thirty", "forty-five" and other composed number words are not recognized. A prompt experiment to fix that (D2) was tried and measured worse on every count, so it shipped as-is: say numbers digit by digit.

three zero for 30 · two seven zero for 270 · four five for 45

Unverified by this pass

Every phrase above was checked against the real matcher with the exact text typed in — never spoken, never through a microphone, never through whisper. Whether saying these words aloud actually produces this text is what today's flight is for.

Bring back

  • Any phrase that didn't do what's expected — say exactly what you said and what happened.
  • Did "to" (#5) and "alright" (#7) feel natural to say, or like you were performing for the matcher rather than talking normally?
  • Anything that surprises you is worth more than anything on this list.
