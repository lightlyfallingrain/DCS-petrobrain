## Part 1 — calibration probe (air start, ~3¾ min)

Setup as probe runs 9–11: Mi-24P air start, guidance unit warm, autopilot on, straight and level,
**overlay OFF**, **no joystick axes bound to the operator's controls**. From the copilot seat,
OBSERV on last (= T+0); be in the **pilot seat by 0:20 and stay there** — no 9K113 view. Hands off
the sight. Back up `Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\Export.lua` first and copy the probe over it.

| T+ | block | probe does | pilot does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:20 | settle | mark to boresight, tiny wobble | — |
| 0:26–0:44 | **A** | mark sweeps slowly right, left, back (~0.3°/s) through boresight | — |
| 0:44–1:35 | **E1, E2** | elevation: a short test pulse, then the mark slowly up, down past the HUD field edge, back | — |
| 1:35–2:07 | **E3** | elevation closed loop: mark held, swept up/down through boresight | — |
| 2:11–2:35 | **B** | no command | **~5° heading change right, back; then ~3° pitch up, back** |
| 2:39–3:04 | **D** | holds a world-fixed direction | **gentle ±3° heading changes** |
| 3:04–3:30 | **F** | short azimuth pulses | **roll into a steady ~20° bank turn at 3:04, hold it** |
| 3:30–3:39 | end | mark back to boresight | **roll out** |

Measures: mark scale both axes (B), azimuth and elevation boresight values from lamp 962's edges
(A, E2, E3), 555's clip edges and whether elevation can be held closed-loop at all (E2, E3), the
azimuth loop's steady error on a world-fixed target vs run 10's 0.06–0.10° (D), and whether the
AI rate adds in airframe or world axes in a banked turn — does 555 move during the azimuth pulses
(F).

## Part 2 — cold start: is arg 960 the guidance unit READY lamp? (production Export.lua or the probe)

Any ground cold start with the 9K113 warm-up done by hand. Note the mission time you switch
`PWR` (ЩО) on, and when the green READY lamp lights. If the probe script is loaded it logs every
lamp change (`lamp 960 READY 0 -> 1`) with the time; the probe's sight steps only start after
OBSERV, so leave OBSERV off during the warm-up.

Expected: 960 goes 0 → 1 about 3–5 min after `PWR`, at the same moment the green lamp lights.
