## What changed from the first probe

Your three points, applied:

- **Switch writes answered and removed.** Several switches moved, so that capability is settled and
  treated as general. **This script never calls `performClickableAction` at all** — only reads, and
  `SetCommand` against the sight's own axes.
- **The warm-up sequence is wrong and is left alone.** You set the aircraft up by hand.
- **Measurement starts at T+60 s**, your number, so the setup window is yours.

The probe reads the six warm-up switches throughout and reports what it found at t=60 s. **If
OBSERV reads off it runs anyway and says so** — a sight that will not move with OBSERV off is a
different finding from one that will not move at all, and both are worth having.

## What it measures

1. **Does the AI azimuth axis still behave as measured on 2026-09-11** — ratio `0.44000`, settled
   inside 0.25 s? Five bearings, each sampled at +0.1/+0.25/+0.5/+1/+2 s.
2. **What does the AI elevation axis do?** Never measured. The gauge is piecewise and its
   degrees-per-unit is unknown, so this is the first real data on it — five commanded values
   against arg 876, well inside the +20°/−15° gimbal whatever the scale turns out to be.
3. **Read granularity of 874 and 876**, three ways: azimuth staircase, elevation staircase, and
   your hand slew. No controller deadband can be finer than that number.
4. **Does the player axis move the head outside the sight's own viewport?** This is the decision.

## What each answer decides

| result | consequence for `BL-B66` |
|---|---|
| player axis **does not move** the head | Take the AI position axis. The controller, the deadband, the tuning pair and the `BL-B63` dependency all disappear — pointing becomes one `SetCommand` per poll. |
| player axis **does move** the head | The rate loop is feasible. The plan's scope caution then applies: it becomes its own entry gated on `BL-B63`, rather than holding up the sight. |
| AI axis ratio **≠ 0.44** | The 2026-09-11 calibration has drifted with a DCS update. Record the version the log prints — every argument number here is positional. |

The summary block at the end of the log states the Q4 conclusion in words, so it can be read
without working through the rows.
