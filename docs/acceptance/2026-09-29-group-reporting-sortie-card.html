Group Reporting Sortie

Last flight (2026-09-28) was ~5 contact reports a minute, the same line up to seven times — 52 individually-resolvable contacts each getting their own callout. Tonight: one line per perceptual group, escalating with range and threat, silent otherwise.

Setup

Three processes, Windows collector already running with --ptt dcs. From run-scripts/ on the Mac:

cd run-scripts
./run-audio-adapter.sh
./run-brain.sh --decider ollama
./run-crew-text-debug-view.sh --brain-client http --brain-url http://127.0.0.1:7796 \
    --detection-trace ~/dcs-group-reporting-trace.jsonl

The trace flag matters tonight — any group that forms wrong, or fails to form, gets a record you can read afterwards instead of relying on memory. --decider stub is fine if you're not testing brain-layer today.

The one that matters

1Is it quieter, and still informative?

Do

Fly a normal leg past whatever's out there — convoy, outpost, scattered armor. Just listen, the way you would on any sortie.

Expect

One line per group, not per vehicle. No repeated near-identical sentences like last time.

2The disclosure ladder

Do

Approach a group from range and let it develop — don't force it by re-scanning.

Expect, roughly in this order
  • Far, undifferentiated"Group."
  • Refined, no threat"Two armor and a truck."
  • Refined, threat member"Danger, ZSU-23-4 Shilka. Also two armor."
  • With clock/range"Group, <clock> o'clock, <range>."
  • No change— silence, deliberately —

3"Pair" vs. "a couple of"

Do

Notice any two-member group Petrovich reports.

Expect
  • Exact, shared type"Pair of T-72."
  • Still undifferentiated"A couple of contacts."
  • Two different things"A armor and a truck."

4Group boundaries

GROUP_REPORTING_COHESION_GAP_UNIT_WIDTHS = 20.0 (~140 m between neighbouring 7 m vehicles) is a stated assumption, not a measurement — your own words were "I have no definitive answer. Pick something, we'll refine it later." Tonight is the first real evidence.

5Known and deferred

A moving convoy passing a static checkpoint may still merge into one group — the mechanism only looks at current spacing, not who's moving together. Common-fate cohesion (Stage 5) is the intended fix and is deliberately not in this build. If you see exactly this, it's expected — record it under test 4 as evidence for Stage 5, not as a new defect.
