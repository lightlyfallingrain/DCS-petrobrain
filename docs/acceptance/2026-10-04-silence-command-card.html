Unbenched — the real question tonight

The three phrases

"silence"   "be quiet"   "shut up"

No recordings exist of you saying any of these — unlike the F10 vocabulary, which went through a full accent-adapted benchmark, these three were only checked against the matcher's text logic. Recognition accuracy on your own voice is unmeasured.

Record

Which phrasing the recogniser actually resolves to silence, and whether any needed repeating. That's cheap for you to notice and expensive for anyone else to guess at from a transcript.

What to expect

Do

Say one of the three phrases.

Expect

"Quiet." — one word — then nothing else at all until your next command. This includes danger calls. If a threat appears while you're silenced, he will not warn you about it — your own choice when this was built, worth remembering at the exact moment you'd otherwise expect to hear him. Text/overlay readouts keep working throughout; only the spoken audio goes quiet.

What ends it

Do

While silenced, give a real command (scan, watch, cancel — anything that actually does something). Separately, talk over him with ordinary chatter that is not a command.

Expect

The real command ends silence and is itself heard normally. The ordinary chatter — the radio-traffic case this command exists for — changes nothing; he stays silenced.

One quirk — not a bug if you hear it

"stop" (the existing stop talking command) also ends silence, because it's a dispatched command like any other — even though its own job is different ("stop talking right now" vs. "say nothing from here on"). Harmless: nothing is queued to interrupt if nothing was ever spoken while silenced. If you hit "stop" while muted and he comes back, that's expected.

Not wired — don't expect this tonight

No DCS F10 radio-menu button for silence. Voice is the only way to reach it today. The dispatcher already accepts the token generically — an F10 entry later is a Hook-script-only change.

Pass criteria

All three phrases are reachable in some form, the ack is heard once and only once per silence, nothing is spoken while silenced — including any threat that appears — and any real command, including an accidental "stop", brings him back.
