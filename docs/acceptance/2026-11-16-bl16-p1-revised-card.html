Read this before you fly A climb of roughly 100–150 m above where you parked, followed by a descent back to it, is the expected, accepted behaviour on one of the two correction paths (see block 4). It is not a bug — don't report it as a regression, just note which path produced it.
Session Operator seat, air start, straight and level, current controls not parked at mission start. One flight covers everything below; park/unpark as many times as you like.
1

Takeover trigger

Do
From the operator (CPG) seat, press "Request Aircraft Control" (key bindings category: Petrovich AI Helper, Multicrew). Do not use any "park/unpark" binding — it does nothing.
Expect
ED's AI takes over immediately. No altitude sink. The command (right) wheel menu opens on its own within a second or two.
Record
2

Menu keep-open

Do
While the AI is flying and the menu is open, close it by hand.
Expect
It reopens on its own within about a second.
Record
3 the prize

Does the altitude actually converge to where you parked?

Do
From the Mac, poll curl http://<windows-ip>:7791/ai_pilot_wheel/latest every few seconds through the takeover. Watch apw_alt_text, apw_last_press, and apw_correction_path.
Expect
The held altitude eventually settles at the altitude you were flying when you pressed "Request Aircraft Control" — not wherever ED's AI happened to set it. apw_correction_path ends as "none", "quick", or "hold".
Record
4

The accepted transient (if the path was "hold")

Do
Nothing extra — just watch the altimeter during the correction if block 3 showed apw_correction_path = "hold".
Expect
A climb of roughly 100–150 m above your parked altitude, then a descent back down to it. This is the accepted cost of working around ED's own bug (a wildly wrong initial barometric target) — not a defect.
Record
5

Unpark

Do
Take control back. Wait a few seconds. Park again (press "Request Aircraft Control" a second time).
Expect
On unpark: the command menu closes, nothing is left half-pressed. On the next park: the whole sequence restarts cleanly, with no leftover state from the first takeover.
Record
