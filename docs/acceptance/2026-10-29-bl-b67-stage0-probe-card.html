A logging-only sortie. It tells the ATGM run what DCS reports when you fire a Shturm, how the pylon knob maps to stations, and whether Radiation Reset can be pressed. No Petrovich this flight.

The production pipeline is off: the probe replaces Export.lua, so no collector, no voice, no Petrovich callouts. Nothing here tests the attack run itself.
The probe moves two switches by itself. In the first ~20 s it sweeps the pylon knob through all 9 positions, parks on the first loaded station and presses Radiation Reset once. After each launch it moves the knob to the next loaded station about 25 s after the shot, never during the missile's flight. Neither can fire or jettison anything.

Flight

Block 1 · first 20 seconds

Hands off the pylon knob

Do
Start the mission and fly straight. Don't touch the SHSCHO knob.
Expect
The knob steps through its positions about 2 s apart, then parks on a loaded station. The Radiation Reset button is pressed once.
Record
Did you see the knob move on its own? Which position did it stop at?
Block 2 · shot 1 The prize

Fire one Shturm at the first BTR

Do
Guide it in normally. Watch it hit. Then wait about 25 s.
Expect
A normal flight. The knob stays put during the flight and moves to the next loaded station roughly 25 s after the shot.
Record
  • Your DCS mission clock at the moment you saw it hit.
  • Did the flight look normal?
  • Did the knob move afterwards, and roughly when?

Why this is the prize: launch and impact for the whole run now rest on DCS's shot event handing over our missile. Nobody has seen that happen for a Shturm yet. Your impact clock is the only way to measure how far the "missile gone" signal lags the explosion you see.

Block 3 · shot 2

Fire a second Shturm at the second BTR

Do
Fire once you're ready. Firing again within 25 s from the same station restarts the knob's wait, which is expected.
Expect
Same as shot 1.
Record
Mission clock at the visible hit. Anything different from shot 1?
Block 4 · optional extras

Ataka, then one pair of rockets

Do
If loaded: one Ataka at whatever is left. If an S-8 pod is loaded: one pair of rockets at anything.
Expect
Rockets are the known case. They only check that the probe is still logging the object feed.
Record
Which of these you fired, and the clock at the Ataka hit if you fired one.
Block 5 · end

Exit to the main menu

Do
Idle a few seconds after the last shot, then exit to the main menu so the logs flush.
