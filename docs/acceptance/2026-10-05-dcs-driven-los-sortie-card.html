DCS-Driven Line of Sight

Petrovich asks DCS directly whether terrain or a building blocks a sightline, instead of approximating it from the stored elevation grid. Buildings occlude for the first time in this project. This card is the only thing standing between this branch and a merge.

In the air

1

dcs.log on mission start

do
Tail dcs.log right after mission start.
expect
PetrobrainLineOfSight lines on a poll cadence with bridge_call_ms, plus look direction set: hour=... fov_half_deg=... lines that change as the scan cycles.
record
Did both line kinds appear? Did the look-direction value actually change over the flight, or stick at one value?
2 new capability

Fly past a building with a target behind it

do
Find a built-up area with a vehicle or AAA positioned so a building sits between you and it, close enough that terrain alone would read clear.
expect
Occluded while the building blocks it, clear once you have an unobstructed angle. Nothing in this project has ever occluded behind a building before — this is the single highest-value thing this card is testing.
record
Did it actually occlude? If not, was the geometry genuinely clear, or did nothing change at all?

4 prize

Read back the real numbers

do
After landing, grep dcs.log's PetrobrainLineOfSight lines for units_in_bubble, units_in_wedge, sightlines_computed, bridge_call_ms.
expect
No prediction — every figure beyond ~10/poll in the plan is an untrusted uniform-azimuth extrapolation. This replaces estimate with measurement.
record
The actual numbers, at a few different moments — open terrain, over a town, near a cluster of units.

Any felt stutter?

6

Watch for silent fallback

do
Check the detection trace for hour_used/fov_half_deg_used being populated through the flight, not null.
expect
Populated whenever the Hook script and look-direction channel both work. If the Hook fails to load, every unit silently falls back to the old SRTM-tolerance primitive — nothing currently alerts on this.
record
Populated throughout, or gaps?
7

Pause-state check

do
Pause mid-flight, wait a few seconds, unpause. This Hook-script family has a history of export-rate bugs invisible to in-flight-only testing.
expect
LOS lines keep appearing or cleanly stop/resume — not silently stale while the sim clock stops.
record
What dcs.log showed across the pause.
8

Engagement calls track the live verdict

do
Watch a contact engage and disengage from behind cover.
expect
Danger/safe calls track the live building/terrain verdicts ("safe from" itself stays deferred, unrelated to this plan).
record
Anything wrong-timed or wrong-direction.
Not tested tonight
  • The atan2 heading convention inside the Hook script's geometry, against DCS's own convention — if wrong, the symptom is a silently mis-aimed wedge (coverage loss), not a wrong detection. Items 1–3 are the only indirect read on it.
  • tonumber("nan"/"inf") behaviour on DCS's bundled Lua — the clamp is written to be correct either way; a crash or stuck look-direction value (item 1) would be the visible symptom if wrong.
  • World-model's offline LOS primitive and its 12 m tolerance — now fallback/fixture only, a separate (WM-B8) question, not a live-flight one.
