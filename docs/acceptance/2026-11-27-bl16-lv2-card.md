## What changed, and why

LV-1 passed ("speed change: pass. proceed to next stage"). The fourth re-fly's own break complaint
— *"Break turn starts fine, but then nose rises too much and bank smooths soon... Could fly breaks
more aggressively, it is an escape manoeuvre"* — turned out to be the ordinary shortfall chain
(pitch-back/bank-easing) firing during a break's own fast roll-in, with no distinction between a
break and any other manoeuvre. Two fixes:

- **A bank/rate cap on the break's own turn rate** (`break_max_bank_deg`, first cut **60°**, about
  2 G — your own figure, 2026-11-27 explore: *"momentary overload accepted, I seem to go to about
  2 G"*). The break's 40°/s rate is now a ceiling, not a target; at 120/180/250 km/h it binds at
  all three (steady rate at the cap: ~32/21/15°/s, 90° in ~2.8/4.3/6.0 s).
- **Height gives first, as long as above 50 m AGL** (your own words, same explore): while a break
  is active, the shortfall that would otherwise go straight into pitch-back and bank-easing is
  instead absorbed by letting the altitude reference sink, in proportion to how much height remains
  above the break floor (`break_height_give_band_m`, first cut 30 m). As height runs out, pitch-
  back/bank-easing phase back in, the same as before this amendment.
- **The break-only floor is now 50 m AGL** (`break_floor_agl_m`), not the ordinary 40 m
  (`floor_agl_m`, unchanged for everything else — U11 answered, *"only breaks"*). Both height
  budgets still apply together: never more than 100 m lost from the break's own entry height
  (`break_max_height_loss_m`, unchanged), and never below 50 m AGL.
- A break still ends exactly the way it always has — reaching its own ±90°/180° heading goal. No
  new state machine, no new way to start or stop one.

## Steps

**1–3. Break left and right at 120, 180 and 250 km/h** (6 breaks total). Voice "break left"/"break
right". Watch per break: steep bank reached promptly, **little to no nose-up**, ball stays
centred, speed stays near entry, height loss ≤ 100 m and never below 50 m AGL, roll-out happens on
the break heading (entry ± 90°). Note whether this reads as *"roll-in good, pull-back gone"*
against the fourth re-fly's own words, or whether pitch/bank still eases sooner than the margin band
would predict — that comparison is this card's real pass/fail, more than any single number.

**4. Break away.** Voice "break away" (entry + 180°). Same watch-items as 1–3.

**5. Low-entry break — both budgets should bind.** From about 70 m AGL, level flight, start a break.
Watch which limit actually arrests the sink: the 100 m loss budget or the 50 m floor — at this entry
height both are close together, so note which one you can see biting first.

**6. Slow-entry break — gentler roll-in, no hand-back.** From about 100 km/h, start a break. Watch:
a visibly gentler roll-in than the 120+ km/h breaks above (U6, the gain schedule at low speed, no
break-specific code) — report if it instead feels the same or more abrupt.

**7. Break started near the rotor-RPM warning (88%).** Right after a hard pull or a high-power
climb (not a dummy toggle — the RPM read is live, `arg_proxy`), while rotor RPM is still sitting
near 88%, call a break. Watch: the easing/height-giving mechanisms do not fight each other —
rotor RPM should not dip toward the 85% floor during the break's own roll-in.
