Known gap — read before you fly

The plan promised ~60–80° of bank at cruise speed on a break. The offline measurement says ~43° at 150 km/h, ~54° at 220 km/h. The heading shaper's gain is an unmeasured placeholder and caps the achieved rate well below the commanded ceiling — raising the rate ceiling alone doesn't raise the bank to match. Flagged for a later retune, not a defect to chase in the air. Expect a gentler break than the plan's text describes, and record the real number.

No standing bank limit exists in mode 1 any more, in any state. If the aircraft rolls further than you expect on any hard turn, that's the removed limit doing nothing — not a bug, but worth recording how far it actually goes.

4 · Low and slow break

ExpectIf RPM dropped under load, the aircraft should accelerate rather than let RPM droop. With the dummy RPM source, this path is barely exercised — "nothing special happens" is itself a useful answer.
RecordWhat the aircraft actually does.
