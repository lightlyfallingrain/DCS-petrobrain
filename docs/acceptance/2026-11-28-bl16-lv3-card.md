## What changed

- **Below 80 km/h the law blends into a hover regime** (λ from horizontal ground speed, sideways
  part included): 80 km/h and up is the LV-2 law; 25 km/h and below is hover; mixed between.
- **Hover:** "speed" is ground speed along the nose, so "speed 0" stops over the ground. The
  **pedals turn the nose**; the bank never turns to a heading. Bank still counters crosswind drift
  (sideways ground speed held at 0). No fixed-spot hold yet — slow drift is expected (accepted).
- **Yaw damper at every speed:** the pedal reacts to yaw the bank does not explain (the nose not
  following the flight path), not only to the ball. This is the fix for the 60 km/h runaway.
- **Hover heading changes are calm:** 45° in about 10 s, at most 8 °/s, no overshoot. During a
  commanded heading change the pedal stays within ±0.3 of the hover-stable pedal; holding heading,
  any pedal.
- **Full pedal range** (the +0.5 right-pedal cap is gone).
- **Below 80 km/h, commanded changes to speed and altitude are gentler**; stabilising corrections
  are as fast as before. The "slowing down = less collective" nudge fades out below 80 km/h.
- A continuous "turn left/right" at hover is a calm pedal turn too.

## Steps

Air start, straight and level, safe height (≥ 100 m AGL; 300 m for rows 1–2 is comfortable).

**0. SAU handover (M4).** On the first voice take: watch the SAU panel. **Expect:** H, K, T go off
and B on, together, at the take. On "I have controls" back: all four on, together. **Record:** did
all four change at once both ways? Any stutter or hitch at the take?

**1. Ordinary flight, damper check** at 150 km/h: heading hold, then a 30° turn each way.
**Expect:** as LV-2 — ball centred, no new yaw or pedal oscillation. **Record:** anything in yaw
or pedal that feels different from LV-2?

**2. The runaway row (the prize).** From 150 km/h: "speed 60", hold 30 s.
**Expect:** the nose stays on the flight path, no left-yaw build or runaway, right pedal comes in
as collective comes in. **Record:** did the nose stay with the flight path? Any left yaw, and when?
How did the pedal feel compared with the sixth re-fly?

**3. Stop.** From 150 km/h at ≥ 100 m AGL: "speed 0".
**Expect:** no jerk; pitch changes gentle below 80 km/h; altitude held; settles over the ground with
slow drift. **Record:** how long from 150 to stopped? Height lost or gained? Did any phase feel
abrupt or too slow?

**4. Hover heading.** At speed 0: "turn heading" +45°, then −45°, then +90°.
**Expect:** calm, about 10 s per 45°, wings level, no nose overshoot. **Record:** seconds per turn?
Did the nose overshoot? Did any turn seem held back (the pedal band limiting it)?

**5. Hover in wind** (if the mission has wind): speed 0 facing into, across, and with the wind.
**Expect:** sideways drift stopped with some bank into the wind; heading held by pedal.
**Record:** how much bank, and did it hold position roughly?

**6. Low hover** at ~15 m AGL (inside ground effect): speed 0.
**Expect:** altitude held as the collective need drops. **Record:** height wander? Any bounce?

**7. Away.** From the hover: "speed 150".
**Expect:** smooth through 25–80 km/h, no lurch where the hover regime hands over.
**Record:** any step or wobble in pitch, bank or pedal on the way through?

**8. Voice take at ~20 km/h.** Hand-fly slow, then "you have controls".
**Expect:** accepted, holds ground speed and heading; a banked take holds heading (bank levels).
**Record:** smooth take? What did it hold?

**9. Break at 180 km/h** with the damper (compare against the LV-2 card).
**Expect:** as LV-2, the damper adding pedal into the turn on roll-in and resisting yaw on roll-out.
**Record:** any difference from the LV-2 break, better or worse?

**Mode 2 sanity (BL-B141, single command counter).** If you park the controls during the flight:
set altitude / speed, align, break via the AI wheel. **Expect:** exactly as before.
**Record:** anything that did not respond, or responded twice?
