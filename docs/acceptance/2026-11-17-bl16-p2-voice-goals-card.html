Voice Goals & HVRT

Petrobrain now takes spoken heading, altitude and speed goals while parked, rotating between them one at a time, and flies a hover translate on command. This card tests the vocabulary end to end: the readbacks, the rotation, what happens when a mode won't cycle, and one rule the main loop decided on your behalf rather than something you asked for in these words — what a translate does to an outstanding goal, and back.

Session 1 — P2a, the goal channel Operator seat, parked (press "Request Aircraft Control" if not already). Air start, straight and level.

One goal, one readback

Do
Say "Heading 3 1 5".
Expect
Immediate readback: "Heading 3 1 5." The aircraft turns toward 315° and the AI wheel presses left/right to get there, settling within about 5°.
Record
2 the prize

Three goals at once — does rotation actually work?

Do
In quick succession say "Speed 2 0 0", then "Heading 0 9 0", then "Altitude 3 0 0" — all three within a few seconds of each other.
Expect
Three immediate readbacks, one per command, in order spoken. All three start converging within the first few seconds (rotation gives each one a ~3 s slice before switching to the next), not strictly one-at-a-time-to-completion. No mode gets visibly stuck or stalls silently.
Record
3

"Unable, not ready" and the retry

Do
Try to provoke a mode-cycle miss if you can (easiest: speak a goal right as the aircraft is already mid-cycle between modes from block 2). If nothing gets stuck naturally, note that and move on — this is hard to force on demand.
Expect
If the wheel can't reach the mode a goal needs within 3 presses, you hear "Unable, not ready." once, then — about 3 seconds later — the AI tries again on its own, with no further mention unless it fails again.
Record
4

Speaking right after parking

Do
Unpark, wait a couple of seconds, then press "Request Aircraft Control" again and immediately say "Speed max" — as fast as you can after parking.
Expect
The readback ("Speed max.") is heard right away — he heard you — but the goal doesn't fight the takeover's own stabilisation; it only actually starts once that settles, a second or two later.
Record
5

Altitude above the radar cap

Do
While on radar altitude reference, say "Altitude 9 0 0 radar" (or any value you know is above ~700 m AGL).
Expect
The readback says "900 meters barometric" — never "radar" — because radar physically can't hold a target that high. The AI actually switches to barometric to get there.
Record
Session 2 — P2b, HVRT translate From the operator seat. Get to a hover first: say "Speed 0" and wait for the AI to slow and transition from forward flight to hover (HVR).
6

Automatic switch into HVRT

Do
Once in a stable hover, say "Left".
Expect
Readback "Left." The wheel cycles itself into HVRT (you'll see/hear the mode-cycle presses), then starts translating left and keeps translating as long as nothing else is said.
Record
7

Hold always cancels

Do
While translating left from block 6, say "Hold".
Expect
Readback "Holding." Translation stops immediately.
Record
Do
Harder case: start a translate again ("Right"), then deliberately let the aircraft drift out of hover (push it forward a touch, or just let airspeed build) so the mode leaves HVR/HVRT on its own, then say "Hold".
Expect
"Hold" still works and still says "Holding" — this is the specific review fix: cancelling a stuck translate must never be gated behind the same hover check that gates starting one, or there'd be no way to stop it short of unparking.
Record
8

Up / down

Do
From a hover (cycle into HVRT if needed, e.g. by saying "Left" then "Hold" first), say "Up", then a moment later "Down".
Expect
Readbacks "Up." / "Down." — each a short 2 m tap, not a held climb/descent like the FLT/HVR altitude goal.
Record
9 confirm or overrule

The arbitration rule: a translate pauses your goals, a new goal clears the translate

Do
Say "Heading 1 8 0" while still in forward flight/hover with nothing else outstanding. Once it's actively turning, say "Left" (an HVRT translate — cycle into HVRT if not already there). Then say "Hold". Then, while still translating (say "Left" again if "Hold" stopped it), say "Heading 0 0 0".
Expect
Starting the translate pauses the heading goal — it keeps its target, it just stops moving while the translate runs. "Hold" lets the heading goal resume moving toward 180°. Speaking the new heading goal (000) clears the translate (releases the held press) and the heading goal takes over.
Record
10

Seat gate

Do
With a goal still active (heading, altitude, or speed — anything mid-flight), switch to the pilot seat.
Expect
Every press stops the instant you leave the operator seat. Nothing keeps nudging the wheel from the pilot's seat.
Record
