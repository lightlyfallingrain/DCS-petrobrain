Blocks

1 · the prize

Does he keep looking while he talks? Prize

Do
With --eyesight-view or --pov-view up, ask for something long: "report contacts" with several groups in belief, or let a multi-line callout run. Watch the view while he speaks. Then try a slew: on OBSERV with a target, "set target …" while a long line is still playing.
Expect
The view keeps redrawing once a second through the whole utterance. Gaze keeps stepping through the scan plan. A 9K113 slew starts without waiting for the line to finish. Before this change the view froze for the length of the line.
Record
Did the view ever freeze while he spoke, and for roughly how long? Did a slew start while a line was still playing?
2

"Stop talking" cuts him off fast

Do
Mid-sentence on a long line, say "stop talking". Do it twice, once while a queued second line is waiting behind the first.
Expect
Speech stops within about a second. No readback. The queued line is dropped, not spoken afterwards. The view never stalls on the command.
Record
How long from the word "stop" to silence? Did anything queued still get spoken after?
3

One mouth: order kept, nothing spoken over

Do
Give two commands back to back, faster than he can answer: "scan left" then "report contacts". Also let a routine callout land while a readback is playing.
Expect
Readbacks come out in the order given, one after the other, never overlapping. A routine callout waits its turn. An urgent callout (SAM launch) still interrupts.
Record
Did any two lines overlap? Did any readback arrive out of order or go missing?
4

Mission restart drops stale lines

Do
With contacts in belief and him mid-callout or with a line queued, restart the mission from the DCS menu.
Expect
The line in progress stops. Nothing from the old mission is spoken into the new one. The first thing you hear is the new mission's own.
Record
Was any pre-restart line spoken after the restart? What was the first line heard in the new mission?
5 · optional, on the ground

audio-adapter down: he keeps working, logs one line

Do
Kill audio-adapter while body-layer runs. Give a command. Wait 30 s. Start audio-adapter again, give another command.
Expect
body-layer keeps ticking (view redraws, commands still show in the console). Log shows exactly one audio adapter disconnected warning, not one per line. On restart, one audio adapter connected and speech resumes with the new command, not a backlog of the old ones beyond what was queued.
Record
How many disconnected lines in the log? Did speech resume on its own?

Bring back

  1. Did the view ever freeze while he spoke, and did a slew start mid-line? (the prize)
  2. "Stop talking": seconds to silence, and whether anything queued leaked through.
  3. Any overlap, reordering or missing readback.
  4. Any old-mission line heard after a restart.
  5. Disconnected-line count with audio-adapter down, and whether speech came back by itself.

Anything that surprises you is worth more than anything on this list.
