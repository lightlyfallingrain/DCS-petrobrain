Columns: time, mode, a_f a_r a_u (m/s², heading frame — forward, right, up), v_g v_g_f (ground speed, and along the nose, m/s), phi_dot theta_dot psi_dot (bank / pitch / heading rate, °/s), ball. Numbers appear in every mode, even before any take.

a Voice take retest (stage 3 alone)

Do
Set a desired state, then “you have controls.” Later, an unpark from mode 2 to mode 1.
Expect

Record

b Level legs: 60 s north, 60 s east

Do
Steady height, steady heading, no turns. One leg on 000°, one on 090°.
Expect
v_g_f ≈ v_g (nearly all of the ground speed is along the nose). a_f, a_r, a_u hover near 0. psi_dot ≈ 0.
Record
On either leg, did anything read rotated or backwards — e.g. v_g_f near zero or negative while flying straight ahead?

c Steady turns: 30° and 60°, both ways

Do
Hold each coordinated, level turn at least 10 s. Left and right.
Expect
psi_dot ≈ turn rate: at 150 km/h about 8 °/s at 30°, 23 °/s at 60° (200 km/h: 6 and 17). Sign follows turn direction. theta_dot stays near 0 (pitch attitude not changing, even at 60° bank). a_u stays near 0 — this is not a G-meter, which would read ~2 g at 60°. a_r carries the turn: roughly 5.7 m/s² at 30°, 17 m/s² at 60°.
Record
Rough psi_dot and a_r at each bank, which sign for left vs right, and whether theta_dot and a_u stayed near zero.

d Speed up, slow down

Do
From level flight, nose down to accelerate, then nose up to decelerate.
Expect
a_f positive while speeding up, negative while slowing.
Record
Sign of a_f each way, and roughly how big.
