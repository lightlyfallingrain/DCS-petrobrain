Branchfeature/bl16-lift-vector-lawrepo root: git checkout feature/bl16-lift-vector-law && git pull, usual redeploy. Both debug flags on.
New deploy step: the LOS Hook changed. Copy aircraft-layer/dcs-export/petrobrain-line-of-sight-hook.lua to Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\Hooks\ (your updated script, or by hand), then restart DCS — Hooks load at DCS start. Without it: no look-ahead, silently; only the 50 m floor beneath holds.
What changed. A 50 m floor in normal flight; a lower height you command is accepted. He reads ground height 1 km ahead along the nose (200 m steps, ±200 m wide, ~1 Hz) and climbs early to clear it at your commanded height. No 5 m/s climb cap: power is the limit, and any climb trades speed down to ~100 km/h, then back. Past a crest he eases down at ≤ 10 m/s. No callouts.
