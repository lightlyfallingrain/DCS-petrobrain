Contact-Report Flood Sortie

Your own words, mid-flight yesterday: "it becomes noise, there is no signal — it also feels like many of those reports were about the same units." You were right about the same units. This branch silences a contact's re-founding when it's plausibly the same real thing you already heard about.

What should sound different

17/34foundings now quiet
4six-vehicle cluster: still speaks
1same cluster: echo silenced
  • Roughly half the first-sighting chatter should be gone, by the sortie this fix was measured against.
  • Genuinely simultaneous distinct sightings are unaffected — they still each speak once.
  • A re-founding of something you already heard about should go quiet.

Deliberately unchanged

"A couple of contacts, eleven o'clock" — group lines are a different event, untouched. And a contact genuinely coming back after really going quiet (CONTACT_REACQUIRED) is never suppressed — if something you lost returns, you still hear it.

The known cost — read this before you fly

A genuine split right after a merge looks exactly like a merge-echo at this layer, and the fix can't tell them apart — so the occasional real "actually that's two things" line can also go quiet. Known and accepted, not a bug: building per-member identity tracking to tell them apart has already been priced and rejected twice. Also: suppression is one-shot and permanent for the sortie — a silenced re-founding is never spoken later, though the contact stays answerable via "report."
