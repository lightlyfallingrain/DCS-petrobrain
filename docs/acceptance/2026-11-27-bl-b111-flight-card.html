Air start, straight and level, 50–800 m AGL. The eyesight view shows where the binoculars look.
What changedA sighting on or beside a group he remembers but can't see now is held. He puts the binoculars on that group's expected place, then decides: second group (“new contact”), the same group (silent, or “that group, now …”), or he can't look and calls it new. Point probe confirmed live today.

1 · Ridge caseThe prize

One group glassed on the far side of a ridge, a second, different group nearer on your side on about the same bearing. Fly so the far group drops behind the ridge for a few seconds, then let the near group come into range.
Expect
Binoculars swing to the far group's last place first (eyesight view), a short pause, then “new contact” for the near group only. Far group not re-announced.
Record
Where did the glass go first? How long was the pause? What did he say?

2 · Parked group, 10+ minutes away

Glass a stationary group once, leave for 10+ minutes, come back on about the same bearing and range.
Expect
No “new contact”. No “that group, now …” unless the clock-hour or 0.5 km range bucket really changed.

3 · SAM site after ten minutes

Same as 2 with an air-defence group.
Expect
At most one threat line over the whole sequence; never a second “new contact” for the same site.

4 · Group in a town

Same as 2 with a group parked in or next to a settlement.
Expect
“New contact” — he can't see into a town, so he can't confirm the place empty. Silence or a relocation line here is worth reporting.

5 · Busy scene

Several contacts in range together for a few minutes. Nothing specific to hear; the log counts how often the hold-and-look ran.
Across every case“That group, now …” should sound like a position update, never “has moved”. A sighting that appears in the eyesight view and then is never mentioned at all is a bug.
Bring back
Answers per case, and the stamped body-layer/logs/expected-area*.jsonl plus the usual sortie logs. Anything that surprises you is worth more than anything on this list.
