## What this checks

LV-1 is the first stage that actually **flies** on the new law: cyclic tilts the lift vector
toward a desired 3D acceleration (feed-forward plus a slow trim learnt from the estimator's own
measured acceleration), collective sets the vector's length the same way, and the pedals fly the
slip ball, always — never a deliberate extra pedal in a turn. **No commanded-speed floor and no
80 km/h hand-back any more** (U1/U8, your own words: *"remove the limit. Any speed. What does not
work, I will bring back and we fix"*) — what protects low speed is the gain schedule alone, which
is exactly what the deliberate slow-flight rows below are for.

## Setup

Air start, straight and level, safe height (300–800 m AGL unless a row says otherwise). Collector
address from `run-scripts/petrobrain.env`:

```sh
source run-scripts/petrobrain.env
while true; do curl -s http://$DCS_COLLECTOR_IP:7791/flight_control/latest | python3 -c "import sys,json; d=json.load(sys.stdin) or {}; print(d.get('t'), d.get('fc_mode'), d.get('fc_measured_heading'), d.get('fc_measured_altitude'), d.get('fc_measured_speed'), d.get('fc_slip_ball_pilot_raw'), d.get('fc_est_a_f'), d.get('fc_est_a_r'), d.get('fc_est_a_u'), d.get('fc_in_band_heading'), d.get('fc_in_band_altitude'), d.get('fc_in_band_speed'))"; sleep 0.5; done
```

Columns: sim time, mode, measured heading/altitude/speed, the raw slip ball (centred ≈ +0.03,
ball right = positive), heading-frame acceleration (`a_f`/`a_r`/`a_u`), and the three in-band
flags.

## Steps

**1. Take into a held heading (E1, E2).** Voice take ("you have controls") in steady, level
flight, no command given. Watch: wings level within a few degrees within a couple of seconds, no
pedal kick, ball settles near its centred reading (+0.03), heading holds steady. No "stupid
attitude changes" on the first frame.

**2. Turns, both ways, two speeds.** 30° and 90° heading changes, both left and right, at 120 and
220 km/h (8 turns total). Watch per turn: ball stays centred through entry/steady/roll-out (pedal
goes in **with** the turn, not held still), collective comes in **with** the bank (same second,
not after altitude starts sagging), no altitude sag on roll-in or balloon on roll-out, settles on
the commanded heading without overshoot you'd call hunting.

**3. Speed changes.** 150 → 220 → 150 km/h, level flight. Watch: accelerating tilts forward and
raises collective together (not sinking first); decelerating tilts back and **lowers** collective
together (not ballooning first) — this is the "slowing down is always pitch back and also less
collective" rule, so collective should visibly ease off as the nose comes up, not stay put.

**4. Climb and descent.** 100 m climb, then 100 m descent, level heading/speed otherwise. Watch:
speed doesn't swing outside its own band through either manoeuvre.

**5. Heading hold in a crosswind.** Steady heading command in a quartering wind (whatever the
mission gives you, or request one). Watch: the **nose** stays on the commanded heading (HSI), the
aircraft is allowed to drift off the ground track — not corrected toward a ground-track line.

**6. `K_L = 0` comparison row.** Edit `flight_profile.lua`'s `k_lift_ff` to `0.0`, redeploy, repeat
one of the turns from step 2 (pick one bank/speed). Watch for a brief sag at roll-in that step 2's
same turn (with the feed-forward on) did not show, then set `k_lift_ff` back to `0.05` and
redeploy before continuing.

**7. Voice take at about 70 km/h.** Slow the aircraft (hand-flown) to about 70 km/h, steady, then
say "you have controls". Watch: accepted — no "unable, too slow" — and a gentle acceleration
toward whatever speed is desired, not a lurch.

**8. Take from a 30° bank.** Hand-fly into a steady 30° banked turn, then say "you have controls"
mid-turn. Watch: the take is bit-exact (no visible jump on the first frame), and the aircraft
settles — bank comes back toward level (or holds a sensible turn if a turn command follows),
never a runaway (a bank that keeps increasing, or a rapid oscillation building in amplitude —
exactly the shape the LV-0 debug pass's retest crash showed: a roll rate that ran away from a
bounded oscillation to several hundred deg/s within about 200 ms). If you see anything like that,
**stop the manoeuvre and take the stick back immediately** and report it in detail (what the bank/
roll rate looked like, how fast it built).

**9. Take mid-turn.** Same as step 8, but from a *continuing*, not momentary, turn (hold the turn
for several seconds before taking, so the aircraft has real turn rate and sideways acceleration at
the instant of capture, not just a static bank). Same watch-items as step 8.

**10. Deliberate slow-flight row — expected to find the edge, not pass cleanly.** At safe height
(at least 300 m AGL), steady wings-level flight, say "speed 40". If it settles cleanly (no drift
off heading, no altitude sag the collective loop can't arrest, no hunting in bank or pedal), **only
then** say "speed 20". The forward law has no ground-speed reference and no pedal yaw loop down
here (that's LV-3's job) — watch closely and **take the stick back the moment anything looks
wrong**, rather than letting it develop. Report exactly what you saw and at what speed, even if
nothing went wrong.
