**Branch: `probe/9k113-slew-readback`.** Script: `aircraft-layer/dcs-export/Export.probe-9k113-point.lua`.
Nothing else of ours needs to run.

**Why:** the readback (874/876) can't be trusted in this setup
(`aircraft-layer/research/2026-10-21-9k113-slew-readback-probe-findings.md`), so your eyes are
the measurement. If BL-B80 is built open-loop on the AI axis, it rests on this.

## Run 1 result (2026-10-21): no movement

Pilot, verbatim: *"no movement. The 9K113 lock onto where it is when OBSERV activates, that is
what I saw earlier and thought it was minor movement from us, but was actually airframe moving.
At 1:50 I activated DCS AI Petrovich and commanded forward scan with 9K113. Then it moves."*

So AI-axis writes do nothing while DCS's AI Petrovich is off, and run 3 of the readback probe was
gyro stabilisation, not our write. On 2026-09-11, when both write and readback worked, AI
Petrovich was on.

## Run 2 — with DCS AI Petrovich ON

**Same procedure, plus: switch on DCS's AI Petrovich before the first target**, but do not order a
scan. The probe now logs 874/876 once a second in every phase, including after `PROBE DONE`. So
afterwards you can also order a forward scan and the log shows what his own movement reads.

## Run 2 result: readback works only while AI Petrovich runs; single writes ignored

874/876 read 0 until t = 122, then tracked his forward scan (about ±17°, alternating). Single
centre writes at t = 127 / 137 did not interrupt it.

## Run 3 — AI Petrovich ON, target re-sent every frame

As 2026-09-11 did. **Switch on DCS AI Petrovich before T+0:20, no scan.** Watch whether the sight
follows the targets. Timeline as in the table below (schedule done at T+2:00).

## Setup (Windows)

1. Back up `Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\Export.lua`, then copy the probe over it.
2. Mi-24P on the ground, warm-up done. **Park facing something you can recognise** (a hangar or
   a road), so left/right and the angles can be judged.
3. From the **copilot seat**, switch OBSERV **on last**. Then move to the **pilot seat** within
   20 s.

## What happens

Each target is written **once** and held **10 s**, going straight from one target to the next.

| # | target | what you should see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | centre | the mark at the reference |
| 2 | 20° left | |
| 3 | 20° right | |
| 4 | 40° left | |
| 5 | 40° right | |
| 6 | 60° left (azimuth stop) | |
| 7 | 60° right (azimuth stop) | |
| 8 | elevation +0.5 | up? how many degrees? |
| 9 | elevation −0.5 | down? how many degrees? |
| 10 | 30° right, elevation +0.25 | |
| 11 | centre | |

About 2 minutes. Watch the **ASP-17 mark**. For targets outside its field, also look at the sight
head on the nose: the external view (F2) shows the turret. If you can, check from the sight's own
view once.

## Bring back

- **Per target: did it go the right way, roughly the right amount, and hold for the whole 10 s?**
  Did it jump there, or travel?
- Anything that drifted back toward centre or moved on its own.
- `Saved Games\DCS\Logs\aircraft_layer_probe_9k113_point.log`, for the times.

Restore the production `Export.lua` afterwards.
