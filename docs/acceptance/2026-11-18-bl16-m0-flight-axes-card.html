BL-16 mode 1 · probe M0, sortie 1 · Windows, air start

Flight Axes Probe

Branch: probe/bl16-flight-axes (or main once you say merge)

What this flight decides

Whether Petrovich can fly on the raw controls at all, and the numbers his control loop needs. One question outranks the rest: when the probe holds the controls, does your physical stick still move the aircraft? If it does, mode 1 can't work as designed.

19segments, one trigger
~25 minflight time, both seats
150 km/h~200 m AGL, flat ground

Not in this flight

  • Petrobrain doesn't run. The probe replaces Export.lua, so there's no Petrovich, no voice, no telemetry.
  • Sortie 2 (step response, release, trim, SAU, seat swap while holding) comes later.
  • Controls stay unparked until J. Only the J rows ask you to press Request Aircraft Control.

The one control: ICS half-press

idlehalf-press starts the next segment readA1–A4, B1, C1: half-press ends it writeB2, C2, D1, D2 and J run themselves and end on their own; a half-press stops writing at once

Either stick's ICS works. A full radio press is ignored. C2 also stops by itself if vertical speed passes 8 m/s, or if the collective jumps or moves the wrong way.

0Set up

Do 
repo root, Macgit checkout probe/bl16-flight-axes

Copy aircraft-layer/dcs-export/Export.probe-flight-axes.lua over Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\Export.lua with your sync script. Mi-24P, air start, pilot seat, straight and level at about 150 km/h and 200 m AGL over flat ground.

unverified in DCS

Lua 5.1 syntax checked, and every segment run against stubbed DCS calls on the Mac, including the C2 wrong-sign abort.

Expect

Logs\aircraft_layer_probe_flight_axes.log appears and grows. Nothing in the cockpit changes until you half-press.

ARead only: A1–A4

Do

Four segments, half-press to start each and half-press to end it. You fly; the probe only reads.

  • A1: 60 s straight and level.
  • A2: 20° bank turn left, then right.
  • A3: climb 100 m at about 5 m/s, then descend 100 m.
  • A4: accelerate 150 → 220 km/h, then back.
Expect

Nothing visible. The log records every heading, speed, height and rate source each frame.

Record
  • What does the HSI read each time you end A1, A2 and A4? Three numbers.

BPedals: B1 read, B2 write

Do
  • B1: sweep the pedals slowly full left, full right, back to centre. Half-press to end.
  • B2: feet off the pedals, then half-press. The probe writes the pedal command: where you left them, then +0.05, −0.05, +0.10, −0.10, back, 3 s each. 18 s, ends on its own. Hold the stick yourself.
Expect

The cockpit pedals move with each step, and the nose yaws one way then the other, the bigger steps more. The log's B2 result says whether D will hold the pedals too.

Record
  • Did the cockpit pedals visibly move in 3 s steps?
  • Which way did the nose go on the first step, and how much did it yaw?

CCollective: C1 read, C2 write

Do
  • C1: sweep the collective slowly low → high → back to where it was. Half-press to end.
  • C2: hand off the collective, then half-press. The probe holds it where you left it for 3 s, then +0.02, back, −0.02, back, 2 s each. 11 s, ends on its own, or stops itself if anything looks wrong. Fly the cyclic yourself.
Expect

No jump at the start. A small climb on one step and a small sink on the other, well under 8 m/s. If the sign guess is wrong, the probe stops writing within about a second and the log says why.

Record
  • Did the collective lever jump when C2 started?
  • Did vertical speed go up or down on the first step?

DStir the controls: D1, D2go / no-go

Do

Half-press for D1. The probe holds pitch and roll where they are, plus the pedals and collective if B2 and C2 passed, for 30 s. Meanwhile, deliberately stir the stick for about 10 s, then the pedals for 10 s, then the collective for 10 s. Don't fight it hard; move them the way you would by accident. Then the same for D2, which holds with a much smaller dither.

Expect

The aircraft keeps its attitude while you stir, and the cockpit stick shows the held position, not yours. If the aircraft follows your hands, half-press to stop: that's the no-go answer.

Record
  • Did the aircraft respond to your stick, pedals or collective at all while held? Which ones?
  • Any twitch or shimmer in D1 that D2 didn't have, or the other way round?

OB, C and D again, from the operator seat

Do

Move to the operator seat with the copilot controls unparked: Petrovich would be flying from the pilot seat while you sit up front. Then run the six segments B1o, B2o, C1o, C2o, D1o and D2o exactly as above, using the operator stick's ICS.

Expect

The same as from the pilot seat. This is the first time pedal, collective and the stirred-stick hold are measured from the front seat.

Record
  • Anything that behaved differently from the pilot-seat pass?
  • D1o/D2o: did the aircraft follow your operator stick, pedals or collective while held?

JPark and unpark while held: J1, J2, J3

Do

Stay in the operator seat. Each J row starts with the probe holding the controls.

  • J1: half-press, then press Request Aircraft Control to park. The probe stops writing the moment ED's AI takes over. Let the AI fly about 20 s, then press it again to unpark. In that same frame the probe takes the controls back and holds for 10 s.
  • J2: the same again. The probe uses a different starting position this time.
  • J3: half-press, park, then swap to the pilot seat while parked. The probe takes the controls back at the swap and holds 10 s.
Expect

No lurch at the unpark or the swap. The aircraft carries on from what ED's AI was doing, and the cockpit stick holds still instead of following yours. If nothing happens within 2 minutes the row gives up by itself.

Record
  • At each unpark, and at J3's swap: did the aircraft jump, and which way?
  • Was J1 or J2 smoother?

ERestore and bring back

Do

Copy the production aircraft-layer/dcs-export/Export.lua back over Saved Games\DCS\Scripts\Export.lua. Don't restore an old backup. Drop Saved Games\DCS\Logs\aircraft_layer_probe_flight_axes.log into win-mac-sync/from-windows/dcs-logs/.

Bring back

  1. The log file, in win-mac-sync/from-windows/dcs-logs/.
  2. D, in both seats: did the aircraft follow your stick, pedals or collective while held?
  3. J: any jump at the unparks or the seat swap? J1 or J2 smoother?
  4. The three HSI headings from A.
  5. B2: did the pedals step, and which way did the nose go?
  6. C2: any jump at the start, and which way did VS go?
Anything that surprised you is worth more than anything on this list.
