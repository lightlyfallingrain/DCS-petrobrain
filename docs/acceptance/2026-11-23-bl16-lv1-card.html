BL-16 lift-vector law, stage LV-1 — first flight on the new mode 1 law, plus the 2026-11-23 handback fixes — 2026-11-24

Branch feature/bl16-lift-vector-law on both machines — git checkout feature/bl16-lift-vector-law && git pull, then your usual redeploy and restart of collector and body-layer. Back with git checkout main.
New in mode 1: he steers the flight path (DCS velocity), pedals always fly the ball, slowing is cyclic back and less collective, every slow trim is capped, the take starts from where your stick is. No minimum speed, no “too slow” — your “any speed, what does not work I will bring back”. RPM, blade-stall and VRS guards stay.

Not on this card: breaks (LV-2), hover and low-speed regime (LV-3), “stand by” readbacks (BL-B134), the 5 s overlay-release window. A command during the second after a mission restart still gets “unable, not ready” — expected for now.

If anything diverges

A bank that keeps growing, or an oscillation getting bigger: “I have controls” and take the stick at once. Note the mission time. That shape crashed the last retest within 0.2 s; LV-1 is built and tested against it, this card is the live check.

R1 Take after a mission restart

Do
With body-layer already running, restart the mission. Wait ~5 s, then “you have controls”.
Expect
He takes it. No “unable, not ready” (unless you speak in the first second after load). No body-layer restart needed.
Record
Taken or refused? What did he say?

1 Take into a held heading

Do
Steady level flight, “you have controls”, no command.
Expect
No jump on the take. Wings level within a few degrees in a couple of seconds, no pedal kick, ball near +0.03, heading steady.
Record
Any jump? How long to settle? Ball and heading after.

8–9 Take from a bank, and mid-turn The prize

Do
(8) Hand-fly a steady 30° bank, “you have controls”. (9) Hold a turn for several seconds first, then take mid-turn. Repeat one from mode 2 → mode 1 (unpark while he flies the overlay).
Expect
No jump at the take. Bank comes back toward level and settles. Never a runaway or a growing oscillation.
Record
Each take: jump or not, settled or not, and if it diverged — what it looked like, how fast, mission time.

2 Turns: 30° and 90°, both ways, 120 and 220 km/h

Do
“turn left/right 30”, “turn left/right 90” at each speed (8 turns).
Expect
Ball centred through roll-in, turn and roll-out; pedal goes with the turn. Collective comes in with the bank, same second. No sag on roll-in, no balloon on roll-out. Settles on the heading without hunting.
Record
Per turn: ball, pedal timing, collective timing, height change, overshoot.

3 Speed 150 → 220 → 150

Expect
Speeding up: nose down and collective up together. Slowing: nose up and collective down together — no balloon.
Record
Did collective ease off as the nose came up? Any height change?

4 Climb 100 m, descend 100 m

Expect
Speed stays near the set speed through both.
Record
How far did speed swing?

5 Heading hold in a crosswind

Expect
Nose stays on the heading (HSI); the aircraft drifts with the wind — not corrected to a ground track.
Record
Nose on heading? Any weaving?

7 Take at about 70 km/h

Expect
Accepted, no “too slow”. Gentle acceleration to the set speed, no lurch.
Record
Accepted? How did the acceleration feel?

R2 Handback with a seat change

Do
While he flies mode 1: “I have controls” and move to the pilot seat before he answers.
Expect
“You have controls” only once he has let go — and then the stick is yours, nothing moving it. If he could not let go, he must not say it.
Record
What he said, and whether anything still moved the stick after it. Mission time.

10 Slow flight — expected to find the edge

Do
At least 300 m AGL, wings level: “speed 40”. Only if that settles cleanly: “speed 20”.
Expect
Not designed for this yet (no ground-speed reference, no pedal yaw loop until LV-3). Take the stick the moment it drifts off heading, sags, or hunts.
Record
How far it got, what went wrong first, at what speed.

6 Optional: lift feed-forward off

Do
In aircraft-layer/dcs-export/Petrobrain/flight_profile.lua set k_lift_ff = 0.0, redeploy, fly one turn from row 2. Set it back to 0.05 and redeploy.
Expect
A brief sag at roll-in the normal turn did not have.
Record
Sag or not, compared with the same turn in row 2.
