Group Cohesion Sortie

Yesterday's sortie repeated one eleven-unit group callout six times, ~11 seconds apart, only the range changing. That's fixed. This branch also redesigns when contacts cohere into one group, and what gets re-spoken when a group changes.

What should sound different

  • The six-identical-lines defect is gone — range drift alone no longer re-triggers a full re-announcement.
  • Group composition says what changed, not the whole roster again.
  • Articles are gone from class nouns: "Armor and truck", not "An armor and a truck". Hearing the old form is a regression worth flagging by name.

Listen for all five

1A new class joining is spoken

Do

Watch an already-reported group until a genuinely new class joins it (e.g. a truck joins an armor-only group).

Expect

A short delta naming just the new class — "Truck, in the group." — not the roster re-spoken.

2Another instance of a reported class is silent

Do

Watch a group with one truck already reported until a second truck joins it.

Expect

— silence — "One more truck makes no difference," your own worked rule.

3Another air-defence unit is always spoken

Do

Watch a group with one air-defence member until a second one — even a different type — joins.

Expect

A delta is spoken, unlike case 2 — "more air defence is always news," even though it's just another instance of a class already reported.

4A leading-threat change speaks a short delta

Do

Watch a group where the leading (most dangerous) member changes — a more dangerous unit joins, or the leader is lost.

Expect

"Now leading: Shilka." — a short clause, not the whole composition re-spoken.

5A departure alone is silent

Do

Watch a group lose a member with no new arrivals and no leader change.

Expect

— silence — at the group level. (A kill should still be heard from that unit's own callout, just not repeated here.)

Not in this build — don't fly listening for these

Damage/destruction narration ("smoking", "burning") and landmark anchoring ("south of the village") are both real, wanted features, scoped to their own later plans. You asked for both today in conversation; neither is here. Their absence tonight is not a defect.

Approved, but may still sound surprising

Infantry merge eagerly, and can bridge other units into one group
A BTR and a truck can land in the same group as three infantry — infantry has no distance backstop at all ("ok to merge infantry too eagerly," your own direction). If a group looks like it pulled in units that don't belong, check whether infantry is the bridge before calling it a miss.
The S-300 is not one installation, so its parts don't all merge
Only S-125 and Kub sites get the flat 500 m installation cap. An S-300 battery's launcher and radar vehicles cohere — or don't — under the ordinary size-relative rule instead, which may split a real S-300 site into more than one group.
